## Shinkansen

- Tokaido Shinkansen, "Smart EX Service" (JR Central, JR West and JR Kyushu's online booking; the train ticket and express ticket in one; you can ride with a transport IC card instead of a paper ticket). Prices per person, one way, ordinary car, normal season, from the official fare tables dated "as of 1 April 2025", which the Smart EX page says apply to bookings made by 30 June 2027 for travel up to 14 December 2027:
  - Tokyo to Kyoto: Nozomi reserved seat adult 13,970 yen, child 6,980 yen; Hikari or Kodama reserved seat adult 13,650 yen, child 6,820 yen; unreserved seat adult 13,320 yen, child 6,660 yen.
  - Tokyo to Shin-Osaka: Nozomi reserved seat adult 14,520 yen, child 7,250 yen; Hikari or Kodama reserved seat adult 14,200 yen, child 7,090 yen; unreserved seat adult 13,870 yen, child 6,930 yen.
  - Tokyo to Nagoya: Nozomi reserved seat adult 11,100 yen, child 5,550 yen. Kyoto to Shin-Osaka: Nozomi reserved seat adult 2,880 yen, child 1,440 yen; unreserved seat adult 1,450 yen, child 720 yen.
  - Reserved seats cost 200 yen less in the off-peak season, 200 yen more in the peak season and 400 yen more in the busiest season (children 100 / 100 / 200 yen); unreserved seats have one price all year.
  - Source: Smart EX official site, https://smart-ex.jp/en/ (Smart EX Service fare tables for reserved and unreserved seats, read from the official PDFs), checked 27 Nov 2026.
- Smart EX Service rules (same source): book from 5:30 a.m. one year before the travel date until 4 minutes before departure; 1 to 6 people per booking; change the booking as many times as you like, free, before you pass the ticket gate; if you miss your train you can take a later train the same day in an unreserved seat. Smart EX prices change in 2027: bookings made from 1 July 2027, or for travel from 15 December 2027, use new prices (unreserved seats and Hikari, Kodama reserved seats are not affected). Fares bought at the station ticket office are not in these notes.
- More Smart EX Service rules (same source, Smart EX Service product page, checked 27 Nov 2026): you cannot get off at an intermediate station (the rest of the journey is cancelled and you cannot board again); the discount cannot be combined with other discounts; the ticket covers the Shinkansen only, so local (conventional) train fares to or from the Shinkansen station are paid separately, and with some transfers Smart EX plus the local fare can cost more than a standard ticket. Refunds: with an IC card you can cancel before entering the Shinkansen ticket gate (or before collecting a paper ticket); the refund fee is 320 yen per booking before your train departs (any seat type). After departure you cannot cancel it yourself: the refund is made automatically from the day after travel, minus a set amount for each section (reserved and Green Car seats) or 320 yen (unreserved seats). If you have already collected a paper ticket, take it and the credit card used to a JR ticket office. If the Shinkansen arrives more than 2 hours late, a set amount is refunded for each section of the journey.
