# Japan prices: official sources (for Packy)

Approximate prices a first-time visitor asks about, from official pages only (operators, airports, JNTO, city governments, the attraction's own site). All checked 24 Nov 2026. Packy should say "about ¥X" with the checked date and link the page, because fares change. Adult fares unless noted; "child" is usually ages 6–11.

## Lockers and luggage

- JNTO, coin lockers in general: "expect to pay between 300 and 600 yen" (varies by size). Source: https://www.japan.travel/en/plan/getting-around/luggage-storage/, checked 24 Nov 2026. JNTO: luggage delivery "fee varies according to the airport, shipping destination and size".
- New Chitose Airport coin lockers: "Counted every 12 hours. (300 yen, 400 yen, 500 yen, 800 yen, 900 yen)", up to 3 days, "All fare amounts include tax". Baggage Port (large cases, strollers): "200 yen per hour per unit, 100 yen per 30 minutes thereafter", cashless only. Source: https://www.hokkaido-airports.com/en/new-chitose/service/baggage/locker/, checked 24 Nov 2026.
- JR Hokkaido station lockers (PDF, Japanese): guide prices small 300〜400円, medium 400〜500円, large 500〜800円; Sapporo Station small 400円, medium 500円, large 700円 or 800円; New Chitose Airport station 400 / 500 / 700 / 800円; Otaru 400 / 500 / 700 / 800円. Up to 3 days including the first day. The PDF does not say what time unit the price covers. Source: https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/network/locker/locker.pdf, checked 24 Nov 2026.
- Gap: Tokyo Station locker prices. Tokyo Station City's information page lists locker locations and the Suica locker availability link, no prices (https://www.tokyostationcity.com/en/information/).
