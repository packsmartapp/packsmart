# Shinjuku Station guide: verified facts and sources

Collected 22 Nov 2026 for backlog B31 (decision D45). Only facts read on an official page (or on a screenshot of an official page that Ari took on 22 Nov 2026) are listed. Anything else is under "Not verified" and must not be published as fact.

JR East's main site (`www.jreast.co.jp`), Tokyo Metro (`www.tokyometro.jp`) and Toei (`www.kotsu.metro.tokyo.jp`) block cloud servers (Akamai or Incapsula "Access Denied"), so their pages were read from Ari's screenshots. The other sources were fetched directly from the `packsmart` cloud environment.

## JR East station maps (screenshots of `https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/e/stations/e866.html`, "As of August, 2026")

**Platforms (1F):**
- 1–4: Saikyō Line, Shōnan-Shinjuku Line
- **5–6: Narita Express** (also Tōbu Line direct limited express)
- 7–8: Chūō Line (Rapid) for Ochanomizu and Tokyo
- 9–10: Chūō Line limited express for Kōfu and Matsumoto
- 11–12: Chūō Line (Rapid) for Nakano, Tachikawa and Takao
- 13: Chūō-Sōbu Line (Local) for Suidōbashi, Akihabara and Chiba
- 14: Yamanote Line for Harajuku, Shibuya and Shinagawa
- 15: Yamanote Line for Ikebukuro, Tabata and Ueno
- 16: Chūō-Sōbu Line (Local) for Higashi-Nakano, Nakano and Mitaka

**Gates:**
- B1F: East Gate; West Gate; Central East Gate; Central West Gate (6:00 to the last train), also called the Keiō exit
- 2F: Southeast Gate (7:00–24:00); South Gate (for the Odakyū Line); Kōshū-kaidō Gate; New South Gate; Miraina Tower Gate

**Connections:**
- B1F "Free Passage" between the East and West sides, outside the ticket gates; the ticket office (Midori-no-madoguchi) is beside it near the West Gate
- B1F routes for the Keiō Line, the Odakyū Line, and the Toei Shinjuku and Ōedo lines (via the Central West Gate area); the Marunouchi Line and Kabukichō via the East side
- West exit bus stop (map note: an elevator to the ground and an elevator to the Marunouchi Line, B2)
- 2F, New South Gate side: routes to the Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal ("Expressway Bus & Airport Express Bus, 4F") and the taxi stop (3F); an elevator to the Kōshū-kaidō overpass (ground floor) near the Miraina Tower Gate

**Facility symbols on the maps:** ticket offices, the JR EAST Travel Service Center (New South Gate area, 2F), a passport reader and reserved-seat ticket machine, currency exchange (2F, New South Gate area), coin lockers (several), restrooms, accessible facilities, a nursery symbol, lettered elevators A–O to the platforms.

## JR EAST Travel Service Center, Shinjuku (screenshots of `https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/multi/customer_support/service_center_shinjuku.html`)
- Hours: 8:00–19:00 (open all year round)
- Cards accepted: JCB, Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Diners Club, Discover
- Sells passes for the East Japan area and basic fare tickets; hands out passes and tickets reserved through "JR-EAST Train Reservation"
- Tourist information for the East Japan and Hokuriku areas, maps of the Shinjuku area; JR-EAST Free Wi-Fi
- Currency exchange (Travelex): foreign currency to yen and back, 8:00–19:00
- No luggage storage or delivery is listed on this page.
- Location (JR East page text, via search, 22 Nov): "on the right after exiting the New South Ticket Gate"

- Sample journeys (JR East train pages, via search): Shinjuku 10:08 → Narita T1 11:27 (about 80 minutes)

## Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal, "Busta Shinjuku" (`https://shinjuku-busterminal.co.jp/en/service/`, `/en/access/`)
- 4F departures (expressway buses and airport buses), 3F arrivals; 2F pedestrian and station level (JR New South Gate)
- Waiting room 3:30–25:00; information counter 7:00–23:00, "English and Japanese supported at all hours"
- Staffed ticket office: Airport Limousine 4:20–22:50 (other systems differ); ticket machines: Airport Limousine 3:40–22:50
- Coin lockers on 4F, maximum 72 hours; small ¥100 per 60 min (then ¥500 per 6 hours), medium ¥100 per 40 min (then ¥600 per 6 hours), large ¥100 per 30 min (then ¥900 per 6 hours); available from 30 minutes before the first bus to the final bus; a QR code on the receipt is needed to unlock
- **Seven Bank ATM in the waiting room, 3:30–25:00**
- Multipurpose (wheelchair-accessible) toilets with ostomate facilities; nursing room with hot water and a changing stand
- Free Wi-Fi "Shinjuku-Bus-Terminal_Free_Wi-Fi"; convenience store 3:30–25:00
- JR New South Gate (the direct link to Busta): 5:45–24:00 per the Busta access page; outside those hours use the South Gate

## Airport Limousine Bus (`https://www.limousinebus.co.jp/en/timetable/detail/Narita-Shinjuku/?dir=2`, `…/Haneda-Shinjuku/?dir=2`, read 22 Nov)
- Narita: adult ¥3,600, child ¥1,800 from the Shinjuku Expressway Bus Terminal; sample first bus 5:40 from Busta, Terminal 1 at 7:10 (about 90 minutes)
- Haneda: early-morning bus 3:50 from Busta to Haneda (late-night and early-morning fares are higher; the Haneda route page lists adult ¥1,400 by day, ¥2,800 late night)
- The timetable page only showed part of the day, so **last buses are not verified**: link the live timetable instead

## Tokyo Metro, Shinjuku Station (screenshot of the 3D station map, "As of 2025.11")
- Marunouchi Line, station M08, platforms on B2F
- Exits are lettered A, B and D (for example A6–A18, B6–B18, D1–D5); **many are marked "Closed"** (A11, A13, A14, A15, A18, B9, B13, B14, B15 and others) during construction
- ATMs on B1F and B2F; toilets, an accessible toilet and a baby room near exit A8
- Routes to the Seibu Shinjuku Line (B10 area), Shinjuku-sanchōme (B6 area), Odakyū HALC (D1, B16, B18), the Toei Ōedo Line at Shinjuku-nishiguchi (E01, B4F) and "Toei Shinjuku Line / JR / Odakyū / Keiō" (A12 direction)

## Added by the w58 build (fetched from the cloud environment, 22 Nov 2026)
- Odakyū Sightseeing Service Center, Shinjuku West (`https://odakyu-global.com/services/odakyu-sightseeing-service-center-west/`): telephone **+81-3-5909-0211**; also sells SIM cards and eSIMs; location confirmed as "Odakyu Line Shinjuku Station B1, West Underground Exit", with the note that stairs and escalators have moved because of major construction at Shinjuku station. - Busta Shinjuku access (`https://shinjuku-busterminal.co.jp/en/access/`): directly connected to the JR New South Gate ("just 2 mins"); New South Gate open 5:45 to 24:00, otherwise use the South Gate (4 min walk).
- Busta Shinjuku services (`https://shinjuku-busterminal.co.jp/en/service/`): free Wi-Fi needs an email registration; coin-operated phone chargers beside the automated ticket machines on the north side, ¥100 per 20 min. The page says the Seven Bank ATM and information counter are "in the waiting room" but does not give the waiting room's floor in text, so the pages do not say "4F" for the ATM.
- JR East N'EX timetable (`https://timetables.jreast.co.jp/en/2612/timetable/tt0866/0866120.html`): re-read, first 5:55 and the hourly pattern confirmed.
