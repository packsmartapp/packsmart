## Common: cards issued outside Japan

- **Seven Bank ATMs take cards issued overseas** with the Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay, American Express, JCB, Discover, Diners Club (and Cirrus, PLUS, Maestro logos shown) marks; "Some cards with these marks may not be accepted" and "Fees for cards issued overseas vary by the card brand". Service hours by brand: Visa and Mastercard 00:00–24:00; UnionPay, JCB, Discover 00:10–23:50; American Express 00:05–23:50; Diners Club 00:05–23:55. Overseas cards: up to ¥100,000 per withdrawal, ¥30,000 for magnetic-stripe transactions. Source: https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/intlcard/card2.html (checked 23 November 2026)
- Seven Bank: language is chosen after inserting the card (English, Chinese, Korean, Thai, Malay, Indonesian, Vietnamese, French, German, Portuguese); Visa and Mastercard users are asked to choose billing in home currency or yen (Dynamic Currency Conversion); "ATMs may not be available due to system maintenance". Source: https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/intlcard/service2.html (checked 23 November 2026)
- Seven Bank ATMs are "at 7-Eleven stores as well as many other locations", including "major train stations". Source: https://www.sevenbank.co.jp/intlcard/index2.html (checked 23 November 2026)
- JNTO lists Japan Post Bank ATMs ("most of them are in the Post Offices, but you can also find some at train stations ... Service hours differ by ATM. The service is available in English"), Seven Bank ATMs, and Mizuho Bank **branches** for international cards; it also says ATMs often charge for withdrawals and not all are open 24 hours. Source: https://www.japan.travel/en/plan/currency/ (checked 23 November 2026)
- Station ATM details below come from Seven Bank's own ATM locator. The Japanese version of each page carries an extra "その他" (location note) line that the English version leaves out; the notes below are translated from it. Every station listing says "利用時間が異なる場合があります" (hours may differ). Convenience-store ATMs (7-Eleven Heart-in / Antre Marché) are listed as "NOT 24HOURS" with no hours.

### B. Which exit

- **Two sides:** "Kyoto Station has two large exits that lead outside: one on the north side called the 'Central Exit', and another on the south called the 'Hachijo Exit'. These are similar to the 'Marunouchi Exit' and 'Yaesu Exit' at Tokyo Station." "The Central Exit is the one where Kyoto Tower is, but ... the tower is not visible from inside the station." "There is a 'Shinkansen Central Exit' ... different from the 'Central Exit' ... that exit will lead you to the 'Hachijo Exit' instead." Source: https://kyoto.travel/en/travel-inspiration/6-tips-to-keep-from-getting-lost-inside-kyoto-station/ (checked 23 November 2026; already used in w39)
- Gate names on JR West's map: 1F Central Gate; 2F West Gate; 2F Shinkansen Central Gate; Shinkansen Central / East Transfer Gates; 1F Shinkansen Hachijo Gate and Shinkansen Hachijo East Gate; 1F Hachijo East Gate; B2F Underground East Gate. Source: JR West map above.
- **Kyoto Tower:** north side, at the Central Exit (kyoto.travel, above). Kyoto Station Building calls the main north exit the **Karasuma Central Exit (烏丸中央口, "front gate")**. Source: https://www.kyoto-station-building.co.jp/directions/ (checked 23 November 2026)
- **City bus terminal:** "Exit the JR Central ticket gate and go outside by the Karasuma Central Exit (front gate). Go straight and you reach the Kyoto City Bus terminal. On the left (west) are the JR highway bus stops and the Keihan bus stops." Taxis: out of the Karasuma Central Exit and turn right (towards Kyoto Theater and Hotel Granvia Kyoto). Source: https://www.kyoto-station-building.co.jp/directions/ (Japanese; checked 23 November 2026)
- **Airport bus (Hachijo side):** from the Central gate, escalator to 2F, follow the north-south free passage south to the end, down to the Hachijo West Exit (八条西口), down to 1F, cross Hachijo-dori, and the airport bus stop is to the left. From the Underground East gate: underground passage towards "JR, Shinkansen, Kintetsu, Hachijo", up the stairs to 1F Hachijo exit, cross Hachijo-dori to the airport bus stop in front of AVANTI. Source: https://www.kyoto-station-building.co.jp/directions/ (checked 23 November 2026)
- **Subway (Karasuma Line):** from the central concourse take the escalator to B2F and go right through Kyoto Porta south area; from the Underground East gate the subway gates (Central 1 staffed, Central 2, North) are straight ahead/right. Source: same page.
- **Hachijo side and Shinkansen:** w39 already has JNTO saying the Shinkansen Hachijo entrance is best for the Shinkansen; kyoto.travel says the Hachijo Exit is "opposite to the central exit where Kyoto Tower is located" (https://kyoto.travel/en/getting-around/comfortable-access-to-around-kyoto-station-to-ji-temple/, checked 23 November 2026).
- **Higashi Honganji:** "**7-minute walk from the station**" (access section) and, in the body text of the same page, "five minutes by foot from Kyoto Station"; also "Subway Karasuma Line to Gojo Station. 5-minute walk". Source: https://kyoto.travel/en/travel-inspiration/winter-openings/ (checked 23 November 2026). Direction: kyoto.travel's Sanjusangen-do route says "From the central ticket gate of Kyoto Station, walk towards Kyoto Tower on the north side and head north along Karasuma-dori Street ... the intersection of Karasuma-dori Street and Shichijo-dori Street (at the corner of Higashi-Hongan-ji Temple)". Source: https://kyoto.travel/en/getting-around/comfortable-access-to-higashiyama-sanjusangen-do/ (checked 23 November 2026). Use "Central (Karasuma) side, north along Karasuma-dori, about 7 minutes".
- **Sanjusangen-do:** "about 18 minutes on foot" from Kyoto Station via the Central side, Karasuma-dori and Shichijo-dori. Source: same Sanjusangen-do page.
- **To-ji:** on foot from the Hachijo Exit, west on Hachijo-dori; or Kintetsu one stop to Toji then about 7 minutes. Source: https://kyoto.travel/en/getting-around/comfortable-access-to-around-kyoto-station-to-ji-temple/ (checked 23 November 2026)
- **JR Kyoto Isetan / Kyoto Ramen Koji / Hotel Granvia / Kyoto Theater:** Isetan 1F entrance is to the left after the Central gate, 2F entrance on the north-south passage; Ramen Koji: Isetan south lift to 10F; Granvia 2F lobby: escalator on the right after the Central gate, then right; Kyoto Theater: right after the Central gate. Source: https://www.kyoto-station-building.co.jp/directions/ (checked 23 November 2026)
