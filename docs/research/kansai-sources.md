# Kansai Airport (KIX): sources and facts (research for Packy, B54)

Started 25 Nov 2026. The airport's own site (www.kansai-airport.or.jp) refuses automated visitors, even a real browser,
so its facts will come from the owner's screenshots. JR West's and Nankai's sites are reachable from the cloud session.

## HARUKA one-way tickets (JR West, read 25 Nov 2026)
Source: JR West news release "Price Revision for HARUKA One-way Tickets Purchased on or after November 1, 2026"
(30 October 2026), https://www.westjr.co.jp/global/en/pdf/press_20261030.pdf
- Applies to the "HARUKA One-way Ticket" and the "WEST QR HARUKA One-way Ticket". The price depends on the date of
  purchase, not of exchange or travel; tickets bought on or before 31 October 2026 stay valid at the old price.
- Adult prices from 1 November 2026, each way, the same for both products (old price in brackets):
  Kansai-airport to or from Tennoji (Osaka City Zone) 1,600 yen (1,300) · Osaka / Shin-Osaka (Osaka City Zone)
  1,900 yen (1,800) · Kyoto City Zone 2,700 yen (2,200) · Kobe City Zone 2,300 yen (2,000) · Nara 2,200 yen (1,800).
- "Child prices are half the adult prices." Validity and other conditions are unchanged.

## Nankai Line to Namba (Nankai's English site, read 25 Nov 2026)
Sources: https://www.nankai.co.jp/en_railway/rapit/ , https://www.nankai.co.jp/en_railway/access-fromkix/ ,
https://www.nankai.co.jp/en_railway/access-ride/ , https://www.nankai.co.jp/en_railway/access-timetable/ ,
https://www.nankai.co.jp/en_railway/ticket/
- Kansai Airport to Namba: Limited Express Rapi:t "fastest 34 mins.", Airport Express "about 45 mins." Rapi:t is all
  reserved seats; "Upgrade to the Rapi:t for just 520 yen!" (a Limited Express ticket is needed on top of the normal
  fare). Super Seat cars 5 and 6 (3 seats per row), Regular Seat cars 1 to 4; luggage space near each door.
- Buying Rapi:t seats at Kansai Airport station: the Limited Express ticket counter; people who bought online or from a
  travel agency abroad queue on the left, people buying on the spot on the right. A "Rapi:t Digital Ticket" (one way,
  on your phone, no queue) can be bought online.
- Ticket machines have English, Chinese and Korean. "If you plan to change to the Osaka Metro, it is cheaper to use the
  special Osaka Subway to Kansai Int'l Airport ticket." Nankai also lists credit-card tap to ride and digital tickets.
- Fares: adults are 12 and over (12-year-olds still in elementary school pay child fares); children 6-11; ages 1-5
  usually free, but they pay the child fare on reserved-seat Limited Express trains such as Rapi:t.
- From the timetable, Kansai Airport to Namba: weekdays, first train 5:45 (Airport Express, Namba 6:29), last train
  23:55 (Airport Express, Namba 0:39); Rapi:t from 6:53 to 23:00. Saturdays, Sundays and holidays: first 5:47 (Namba
  6:32), last 23:55 (Namba 0:39); Rapi:t from 7:06 to 22:55. Trains stop at Rinku-Town, Izumisano, Tengachaya and
  Shin-Imamiya on the way.
- Namba to Kansai Airport: first train 5:15 (arriving 5:58), last train 23:02 on weekdays and 22:55 at weekends and on
  holidays; Rapi:t from 6:00 to 22:00.
- Other trips from the same page: Namba to Koyasan about 90 min (Limited Express Koya, then the cable car at
  Gokurakubashi); Kansai Airport to Wakayama-shi about 40 min (change at Izumisano to the Limited Express Southern).

## Kansai Airport: station and buses (owner's screenshots of the airport's English site, 25 Nov 2026)
Source: Kansai International Airport official site, https://www.kansai-airport.or.jp/en/ (Access pages; exact page
links to add)

**Railway**
- Two railways serve the airport: JR West and the Nankai Electric Railway (the site links to both timetables and shows
  their service status). On the airport's route map, Nankai runs from Kansai-kuko (Kansai Airport) via Rinku Town and
  Izumisano towards Tengachaya, Shin-Imamiya and Namba; JR runs via Rinku Town and Hineno towards Tennoji and Osaka.
- "Kansai Airport Station is directly connected to the 2nd floor of the Terminal 1 Building and the 2nd floor of the
  Aeroplaza Building." JR and Nankai share the one station.
- Station map: the station sits between North Square and South Square, opposite the Terminal 1 entrances on 2F, with
  escalators up to 4F (international departures) and down to 1F (international arrivals). The Aeroplaza (with a hotel)
  is on the other side of the station. The "Free Shuttle Bus Drop (1F) To Terminal 2 Building" is at the south end,
  by South Square. Terminal 1's 2F is the domestic floor (ANA and Starflyer, JAL and Jetstar counters, domestic
  security check), with a police station at the north end and the "Tasty Street" food area.

**Airport buses**
- Buses to the airport: "Take a bus bound for Kansai International Airport (KIX) from your point of origin. All buses
  arrive at the International Departures Lobby on the 4th floor of Terminal 1."
- Destinations listed (each with its own timetable link): Itami Airport (Osaka Airport, Hotarugaike Sta.); central
  Osaka: Osaka Sta. (Chayamachi, Shin-Umeda City, Senri Newtown, Shin-Osaka), Osakajo and Nippombashi, Temmabashi,
  Kintetsu Uehonmachi (Shinsaibashi), Abeno Harukas, Namba (OCAT), Nankai Namba Sta. (night bus), Universal Studios
  Japan with Nanko and Tenpozan (Kaiyukan), Ibaraki, Senri-New Town; elsewhere in Osaka Prefecture: Hirakata and Keihan
  Kuzuha, Higashi Osaka, Rinku Premium Outlets, Rinku Town and Izumisano Sta. (also a night bus), nearby hotels;
  Hyogo: Amagasaki, Nishinomiya, Kobe Sannomiya and Rokko Island, Himeji and Kakogawa; Nara: Kintetsu Gakuenmae, Nara,
  Yamato Yagi; Kyoto: Kyoto and Kyo-tanabe; Wakayama; Okayama; Shikoku: Awaji, Naruto, Tokushima, Takamatsu.

**ATMs in Terminal 1, by floor** (owner's screenshots of the airport's official interactive map, ATM filter,
https://platinumaps.jp/maps/kix-airport?culture=en&category=atm , 25 Nov 2026; the map shows names and places, not
hours)
- 1F (international arrivals; buses and taxis): Seven Bank ATM, at the north end of the arrivals hall, near exit A;
  Aeon Bank ATM, in the north part of the hall.
- 2F (domestic departures and arrivals): MUFG Bank ATM at the north end; Aeon Bank ATM in the middle of the hall,
  near entrance E.
- 4F (international departures): Seven Bank ATM, by the check-in islands, between counters D and E.
- No ATM shown on 3F.
- Terminal 2 (the map's floors are 1F, departures and arrivals with shops and
  restaurants, and 2F, arrivals): two ATMs, both on 1F: a Seven Bank ATM in the west part of the building and a
  Resona Bank ATM in the east part. None shown on 2F.
- Seven Bank ATMs take overseas cards (JNTO's list, in the Japan basics notes); the Aeon Bank and MUFG ATMs: check
  the bank's own page.

## Arriving at Namba: tourist information (Osaka Convention & Tourism Bureau, read 25 Nov 2026)
Source: https://osaka-info.jp/en/spot/tourist-information-namba/
- Tourist Information Namba is "on the first floor of Nankai Namba Station, which is easily accessible from Kansai
  International Airport": in front of the north exit on 1F of Nankai Namba Station, 2 minutes from Exit 4 of Namba
  Station on the Osaka Metro Midosuji and Sennichimae lines. Open 9:00-20:00 every day of the year; English, Chinese
  and Korean.
- The same bureau also runs Tourist Information SHIN-OSAKA (JR Shin-Osaka Station, 3F) and Tourist Information OSAKA
  (JR Osaka Station, 1F).
