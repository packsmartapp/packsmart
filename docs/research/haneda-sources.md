# Haneda Airport: sources and facts (research for Packy, B54)

Started 25 Nov 2026. Each fact names its source. The airport's site blocks the cloud sandbox, so these come from the
owner's screenshots of the official English pages (25 Nov 2026).

## Train & Monorail (owner's screenshots, 25 Nov 2026)
Source: Haneda Airport official site, Access > Train & Monorail, https://tokyo-haneda.com/en/access/train/index.html

**Two lines serve the airport**
- "Haneda Airport is directly connected to the Keikyu Line and the Tokyo Monorail Line." The airport points to each
  company's own website for timetables and fares.
- From Tokyo Monorail Hamamatsucho Station: 13 minutes to Haneda Airport Terminal 3 Station on the Haneda Express.
- From Keikyu Shinagawa Station: 13 minutes to Haneda Airport Terminal 3 Station on the Airport Limited Express.

**Station names and numbers**
- Keikyu: Haneda Airport Terminal 3 Station (KK16); Haneda Airport Terminal 1·2 Station (KK17), one station for both terminals.
- Tokyo Monorail: Haneda Airport Terminal 3 Station (MO08); Shin-seibijo (between T3 and T1); Haneda Airport Terminal 1
  Station (MO10); Haneda Airport Terminal 2 Station (MO11). The monorail has separate stations for T1 and T2.
- On the route map, Keikyu runs from the airport via Keikyu-Kamata to Shinagawa (JR transfer), and the monorail via
  Tenkubashi and Tennozu Isle to Hamamatsucho (JR transfer, close to Daimon on the Toei Asakusa Line).

**Time required to major stations** (the page calls these estimates: "For details, please contact each public
transportation facility")
- Narita Airport Station: about 1 hour 45 minutes
- Maihama Station (Tokyo Disney Resort): about 50 minutes
- Shinjuku Station: about 50 minutes
- Shibuya Station: about 45 minutes
- Ueno Station: about 40 minutes
- Asakusa Station: about 40 minutes
- Tokyo Station: about 30 minutes
- Yokohama Station: about 30 minutes
- Hamamatsucho Station: about 20 minutes
- Shinagawa Station: about 15 minutes

**Keikyu Line route map**
- Train types shown: Local, Limited Express, Airport Limited Express, and Access Express (via the Narita Sky Access Line).
- Some trains from Haneda run straight through Shinagawa onto the subway towards Shinbashi, Nihonbashi, Asakusa and
  Oshiage, and on to Narita Airport (Airport Terminal 2, Narita Airport) without changing. Others run towards Yokohama
  and Zushi·Hayama.
- The map also shows journey minutes for each train type. Which terminal they count from is not stated, so they are
  not used here; use the "Time required" list above.
- Keikyu Information Center: 03-5789-8686, weekdays 9:00-19:00, weekends and holidays 9:00-17:00.

**Tokyo Monorail route map**
- Three train types: Haneda Express, Rapid, Local. Order of stops: Terminal 2, Terminal 1, Terminal 3, then Tennozu
  Isle and Hamamatsucho. On the map the Haneda Express does not stop at Tennozu Isle; Rapid and Local do.
- Transfers: at Hamamatsucho to the JR Yamanote Line for the city centre (Tokyo, Akihabara, Ueno, Ikebukuro,
  Shinjuku, Shibuya); at Tennozu Isle to the Rinkai Line for Shinkiba.
- Tokyo Monorail Customer Center: 050-2016-1640, 10:00-17:00 every day, including 29 Dec to 3 Jan.

**Ticket gates ("Ticket Gate Information", tabs T1, T2, T3; same page)**
- Terminal 1: the Keikyu Line and Tokyo Monorail platforms are at B1F. On the B1F map: the Sun Tower and Moon Tower
  meeting points, the Keikyu and Tokyo Monorail gates, a passageway to the bus terminal and a passageway to Terminal 2.
- Terminal 2: the Keikyu Line and Tokyo Monorail platforms are at B1F. On the B1F map: International Flights Check-in
  Counter T, the Keikyu and Tokyo Monorail gates, a passageway to the bus terminal and a passageway to Terminal 1.
- Terminal 3, Keikyu Line: "Please note that ticket gates differ depending on your destination. For downtown Tokyo and
  Yokohama: The ticket gates are located in Arrival Lobby on the 2nd floor. For T1 and T2, the ticket gates are located
  on the Keikyu B2F."
- Terminal 3, Tokyo Monorail: "The ticket gates are located on the 2nd floor Arrival Lobby and the 3rd floor departure
  lobby."
- Terminal 3 map: check-in counters A to O, Security Check Point (North) and (Central), and the Keikyu Line and Tokyo
  Monorail entrances at the end of the hall, the Keikyu side next to Group Counter Z, the monorail side next to Group
  Counter Y.
- The same information is also on RakuRaku Odekake-net (link on the page).
