## JR East station map (owner's screenshots, 23 Nov 2026; map marked "As of November, 2026")
Source: https://www.jreast.co.jp/en/e/stations/e204.html (floor tabs 1F-M2, 2F, 3F, B4-B1)

**Exits and gates named on the map**
- 1F: Central Gate (yellow, by the main ticket hall), Main Exit, Asakusa Exit, Yamashita Exit, Iriya Exit,
  "for Taxi Stop" (by the Main Exit), "for Tokyo Metro" (two signs, by the Central Gate area), "Transfer to Shinkansen" gate inside.
- M2: Shinobazu Gate.
- 3F: Park Gate and Park Exit (top of the map, "Tickets" and "Fare Adjustment" beside it), Iriya Gate,
  Panda Bridge and Panda Bridge Exit ("for Ueno Park" at the end of Panda Bridge), Statue of Panda.
- 2F: "for Panda Bridge Exit (3F)", "for Iriya Exit", "for ... Hibiya Line" (cut off at the edge: Ginza & Hibiya Line), Lost & Found.

**Platforms (tracks)**
- 2F, tracks 1-12:
  1 Keihin-Tohoku Line (for Akabane, Urawa & Omiya) · 2 Yamanote Line (for Tabata, Ikebukuro & Shinjuku) ·
  3 Yamanote Line (for Akihabara, Tokyo, Hamamatsucho & Shinagawa) · 4 Keihin-Tohoku Line (for Kamata, Kawasaki, Yokohama & Kannai) ·
  5 Utsunomiya Line, Takasaki Line (for Akabane, Omiya, Utsunomiya & Takasaki) · 6 Joban Line, Joban Line (Rapid), Narita Line, Utsunomiya Line, Takasaki Line ·
  7 Ueno-Tokyo Line (for Tokyo, Shinagawa, Yokohama, Odawara & Atami) · 8 Joban Line (for Kashiwa, Tsuchiura, Mito & Iwaki), Ueno-Tokyo Line (for Tokyo, Shinagawa ...) ·
  9 Joban Line, Joban Line (Rapid), Narita Line, Ueno-Tokyo Line · 10-12 Joban Line, Joban Line (Rapid), Narita Line (for Matsudo, Toride, Mito & Narita).
- 1F, tracks 13-17: 13-15 Utsunomiya Line, Takasaki Line (for Akabane, Omiya, Utsunomiya & Takasaki) ·
  16 Joban Line (for Kashiwa, Toride, Tsuchiura & Mito), Utsunomiya Line, Takasaki Line · 17 Joban Line (for Kashiwa, Toride, Tsuchiura & Mito).
- B4-B1, Shinkansen tracks 19-22: 19 and 20 Tohoku, Yamagata, Akita, Hokkaido, Joetsu & Hokuriku Shinkansen · 21 and 22 Shinkansen (for Tokyo).
  Shinkansen concourse has a waiting room and "Tokimeki Square".
- Note for the guide: JR's **Narita Line** here goes to Narita *city* (via Matsudo, Toride); for **Narita Airport** use the
  Keisei Skyliner from Keisei Ueno station (to be confirmed from Keisei's own pages) or JR's Narita Express (not from Ueno; check JR East).

**Facilities on the map (legend icons)**
- Ticket Office and JR EAST Travel Service Center: 1F, by the Central Gate ticket hall.
- Passport Reader & Reserved Seat Ticket Vending Machine: 1F, by the Central Gate tickets.
- Question & Answer: 1F, beside the Central Gate.
- Coin lockers: several on 1F (near the Main Exit / taxi side, inside the gates by the Shinkansen transfer),
  2F (along the concourse to the Iriya Exit), 3F (by the Panda Bridge Exit and near Iriya Gate).
- Lost & Found: 2F, near the Panda Bridge Exit stairs.
- Elevators lettered A to I (A-F to platforms 1-12 from 3F; G and H to the Shinkansen platforms; I to the 2F outside-gates concourse).
- Shops: atre Ueno (outside the gates, 1F-2F), ecute Ueno (inside the gates, 3F), NewDays kiosks.

## Tokyo Metro Ueno Station page (owner's screenshots, 23 Nov 2026)
Source: https://www.tokyometro.jp/lang_en/station/ueno/index.html
- Ginza Line G16, Hibiya Line H18. Address: 3-19-6 Higashiueno, Taito-ku, Tokyo. Station office 03-3832-6629.
- Transfers listed: East Japan Railway (JR East) and Keisei Electric Railway.
- Lost items: same day, ask the station office where the item was lost; next day or later, the Lost and Found at Iidabashi Station
  (Namboku Line) or the Tokyo Metro Customer Relations Center.
- Multi-functional ticket machines at all ticket offices, first train to last train.
- Daily average passengers 2025: 191,804 (10th of 130 stations), excluding passengers on directly connected lines.
- The page screenshots do not include the exit list.
- Metro Pass Office locations (owner's screenshot, 23 Nov 2026): Ueno is one of the stations with a Tokyo Metro Pass Office
  (others on the map: Oji, Ayase, Kotake-mukaihara, Ikebukuro, Takadanobaba, Iidabashi, Otemachi, Kinshicho, Urayasu, Shinjuku, Ginza, Toyosu).
  Pass offices are for commuter passes; whether they sell visitor tickets is not stated on this map.
- Decision 23 Nov: the guide goes ahead without Tokyo Metro exit numbers; it routes people by the JR exits (Park Gate and Panda Bridge
  for Ueno Park). Add Metro exits later only if the owner finds Tokyo Metro's exit list.

## Ueno Park, official site (Tokyo Metropolitan Government, Bureau of Construction; fetched 23 Nov 2026)
- Access page https://www.kensetsu.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/jimusho/toubuk/ueno/access : JR Yamanote, Keihin-Tohoku, Takasaki and Utsunomiya lines
  "Ueno", 2 minutes' walk; Tokyo Metro Ginza and Hibiya lines "Ueno", 4 minutes; Toei Oedo Line "Ueno-okachimachi", 5 minutes;
  Keisei Main Line "Keisei Ueno", 1 minute. Address: Ueno Park and Ikenohata 3-chome, Taito-ku.
- About the park https://www.kensetsu.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/jimusho/toubuk/ueno/kouenannai : open 5:00 to 23:00 (no entry outside these hours);
  about 1,200 cherry trees among the main plants; opened 1873; one of Japan's first designated parks. Park map PDF linked there.
- Highlights https://www.kensetsu.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/jimusho/toubuk/ueno/midokoro : "one of Japan's best-known cherry blossom spots";
  cherry blossoms for about two months, from early-flowering kanzakura to Somei-yoshino; lotus on Shinobazu Pond at its best in late July.
- Rules https://www.kensetsu.metro.tokyo.lg.jp/jimusho/toubuk/ueno/rules : no fireworks, no catch-ball (general rules page).
