# New Chitose Airport (CTS) and Sapporo Station: sources and facts (research for the CTS and Sapporo guides)

Started 24 Nov 2026. Each fact names its source; all fetched 24 Nov 2026 from the cloud sandbox unless marked.
**The airport's own site is blocked:** every `www.new-chitose-airport.jp` URL now 301-redirects to
`www.hokkaido-airports.com/rd_newchitose/...`, and the sandbox proxy refuses that host (403). So terminal floors,
facilities and winter-operations facts from the airport itself are all under Gaps. What we have about the terminals comes
from JR Hokkaido (station maps) and the Sapporo Tourist Association.
Local copies of every page, PDF and map are in this folder (file names given in brackets).
**Update (later on 24 Nov 2026):** `www.hokkaido-airports.com` was then allowed; the airport facts are in the section
"New Chitose Airport, official site" below, which overrides the second-hand airport facts in the first section.

## NEW CHITOSE AIRPORT

### 1. Terminals and how they connect
- JR route guide PDF "Route guide from New Chitose Airport International Terminal to JR New Chitose Airport Station"
  (PDF dated Feb 2024): Source: https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/pdf/e-chitose.pdf (linked from
  https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/english/travel/index.html as "From New Chitose Airport to New Chitose Airport Station"), fetched 24 Nov 2026. [jrh_e-chitose.pdf, jrh_echitose_hi-1.png]
  - Step 1 "International Flight Terminal: Arrives at the Arrival Lobby on 2F."
  - Step 2 "Head down the 240 meter long Connecting Walkway to the Domestic Terminal." (The overview map on the same
    PDF labels the walkway **300m**. The PDF contradicts itself, so say "a few hundred metres" or ask the owner which to use.)
  - Step 3 "Domestic Terminal: Proceed straight through."
  - Step 4 "You will see the Domestic Terminal Elevator after you pass the souvenir shops."
  - Step 5 "Get on the Elevator in the Domestic Terminal and go to B1F."
  - Step 6 "The JR Information Desk is located to the right of the elevator on B1F."
  - Step 8 "JR New Chitose Airport Station Ticket Gate. Platforms are on B2F."
  - Map shows Customs and Immigration in the International Flight Terminal Building, then the Connecting Walkway north to the Domestic Flight Terminal Building.
  - No walking time is given in the PDF. (Gap: the airport states a walking time on its own floor-map pages.)
- JR station map (Japanese, "2026年9月現在" as of Sep 2026): Source: https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/network/station/images/9040.jpg
  (data from https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/network/station/csv/station.csv, the data file behind the JR station search page
  https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/network/station/station.html), fetched 24 Nov 2026. [jrh_station_9040.jpg]
  - Exits up from the station concourse are signed: "2階:国内線出発ロビー / 国際線連絡通路へ" (2F: domestic departures lobby /
    to the international connecting walkway), "1階:国内線到着ロビーへ" (to 1F domestic arrivals lobby), and from the central
    elevator "3階:国際線連絡通路へ" (3F: to the international connecting walkway).
  - (Note: the 2024 PDF says arrivals use the walkway on 2F; the 2026 map also shows a 3F route to the walkway from the central elevator. Both may be true; the airport's floor map would settle it.)
- Sapporo Tourist Association: "General information centers and information centers are located on the first, second, and
  third floors of both the international and domestic terminals." Rental car counters "on the first floor of the domestic
  terminal"; "Rental car guidance is provided at the Transportation Information Counter in the international terminal."
  Taxi: "taxi stands in two locations outside the domestic terminal exits and at one location outside the international terminal exit".
  Source: https://www.sapporo.travel/en/info/about/transportation/, fetched 24 Nov 2026. [trav_transportation.html]

### 2. JR New Chitose Airport Station and the Rapid Airport train
Main source: JR Hokkaido "New Chitose Airport ↔ Sapporo ↔ Otaru (Airport Train)",
https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/english/travel/airport.html, fetched 24 Nov 2026. [jrh_english_travel_airport.html]
- Headline points: "No reservation required for most seats"; "IC cards such as Suica can be used";
  "33 mins to Sapporo / 73 mins to Otaru (fastest)".
- "Direct service between New Chitose Airport, Sapporo, and Otaru."
- Frequency, exact wording: "There are six Airport Trains per hour between 9:00 and 16:00 from New Chitose Airport to
  Sapporo: 1 Special Rapid train (fastest: 33 mins), 3 Rapid trains (fastest: 37 mins), and 2 Semi-Rapid trains (fastest: 43 mins)".
  "Some trains run directly from New Chitose Airport to Otaru."
- Station numbers and stops: AP15 New Chitose Airport (departures for Special Rapid, Rapid, Semi-Rapid and Local);
  H14 Minami-Chitose (all types stop); H13 Chitose (Rapid, Semi-Rapid, Local); H05 Shin-Sapporo (all types);
  01 Sapporo (all types); S07 Teine (Special Rapid, Rapid, Local); S14 Minami-Otaru and S15 Otaru (Special Rapid, Rapid, Local).
- "For Yoichi, Kutchan, Niseko IC cards such as Suica cannot be used. Please purchase your ticket at the station before boarding."
- Seats: "Cars 1-3 and 5-6 Non-reserved Seats ... No reservation required". "Car 4 only 43 seats Reserved Seats ...
  A basic fare ticket and a reserved seat ticket are required". "Passengers without a reserved seat ticket are not allowed
  to enter car 4." "Look for the 'U seat' sign" (the reserved car).
- IC cards listed: Suica, PASMO, TOICA, manaca, ICOCA, PiTaPa, SUGOCA, nimoca, Hayakaken, Kitaca.
- Paper tickets: ticket vending machine "Cash only"; reserved-seat ticket machine and ticket counter "Cash and credit cards accepted".
  "Only cash and credit cards are accepted. QR code payments are not supported." "Tickets are valid for one ride only. If you
  exit the ticket gate during your trip and board a train again, please purchase a new ticket." "With non-reserved seats, you
  can board any Airport Train on the same day."
- Online reserved seats through "JR-EAST Train Reservation"; "Reserved Seat E-tickets cannot be used at ticket gates. Please use
  an IC card such as Suica or a paper basic fare ticket to go through the gate."
- Luggage: "must be within 250 cm (maximum length: 2 m), up to 2 items, each weighing no more than 30 kg *Some items may not be allowed".
- Transfers: at Minami-Chitose for Hakodate (incl. Shiraoi, Noboribetsu, Toya) or Kushiro (incl. Tomamu, Obihiro); "At
  Minami-Chitose Station, limited express trains depart from the same platform. All seats on the limited express trains are
  reserved." At Sapporo for Otaru: "Trains bound for 'Teine' or 'Hoshimi' do not continue to Otaru Station." At Otaru for
  Yoichi, Kutchan, Niseko: "IC cards such as Suica cannot be used beyond Otaru."
- Platforms: station map (Sep 2026, [jrh_station_9040.jpg]) shows **tracks 1 and 2**, with "←札幌・小樽方面" (for Sapporo, Otaru)
  at the end. The route PDF says "Platforms are on B2F" and the ticket gate and JR Information Desk are on B1F.
- Station services (station.csv row 9040, fetched 24 Nov 2026): みどりの窓口 (JR ticket office) 6:30–22:00; ツアーカウンター
  (tour counter) 9:00–17:30; reserved-seat and "talking" ticket machines 6:30 until the last train departs (operator
  support 6:30 to the last train, at most until 23:00). Map legend shows Information Desk (外国人デスク, foreign visitors desk),
  ticket office and tour counter to the left of the ticket gates, coin lockers, waiting space, toilets, nursing room and AED in the concourse.
- JR Information Desk (for foreign visitors): "Help in three languages (English, Chinese and Korean) is available at JR Sapporo
  Station West Exit and at New Chitose Airport Station. (There are cases in which your desired language may not be available.)
  You can exchange the voucher for the rail pass and purchase rail passes and various kinds of train tickets."
  Source: https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/english/travel/info.html, fetched 24 Nov 2026. Hours at the airport station:
  "JR Information Desk Hours: 8:30 - 19:00" (footer of e-chitose.pdf, Feb 2024). The undated map on the info page,
  https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/images/travel/info/e-map02.gif, also says "Office Hours 8:30-19:00" and shows the desk on B1F,
  to the left of the Ticket Gate, with a Waiting room on the right. [jrh_info_e-map02.gif]
- Coin lockers at New Chitose Airport station (JR locker list PDF, updated Jul 2026): small 6, medium 9, large 3 + large 1.
  Source: https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/network/locker/locker.pdf, fetched 24 Nov 2026. [jrh_locker.pdf]
- Sapporo Tourist Association (secondary, older wording): "JR Hokkaido New Chitose Airport Station is directly connected with
  the first floor basement of the airport terminal building." It also says "approximately every 12 minutes ... from after 8:00 to
  after 22:00" and "37 minutes". **Use JR's own wording above instead** (six per hour 9:00–16:00; fastest 33 min).
  Source: https://www.sapporo.travel/en/info/about/transportation/, fetched 24 Nov 2026.
- First and last trains: **not found** (see Gaps).

### 3. Airport buses to Sapporo
- Operators: Hokkaido Chuo Bus (中央) and Hokuto Kotsu (北都). Both run alternating departures on the same route table.
  Source: Chuo Bus airport timetable "時刻表（千歳発＞札幌行き）" (Chitose to Sapporo), "■2026年4月1日更新（夏ダイヤ）"
  (summer timetable, updated 1 Apr 2026): https://www.chuo-bus.co.jp/airport/timetable/?ope=det&n=34&o=2&t=14, fetched 24 Nov 2026. [bus_tt_34_2.html]
  **This is the summer timetable. A winter timetable may replace it; check before publishing.**
- Airport stops (boarding): "新千歳空港ＡＮＡ前: 22番乗り場" (New Chitose Airport ANA Gate, stop 22); "新千歳空港ＪＡＬ前:
  14番乗り場" (JAL Gate, stop 14); "新千歳空港・国際線（タ）: 84番乗り場" (International Terminal, stop 84); then Minami Chitose Sta.
- Sapporo-city stops on the main route (札幌都心, "Sapporo city centre"), in order: ... Minami3jo-Susukino, Odori Park,
  Hotel Monterey Edelhof, ANA Crowne Plaza Hotel Sapporo, **Sapporo Sta. (札幌駅前)**, Hokkaido Government, several hotels, to
  Grand Mercure Sapporo Odori Park.
- Timings computed from that timetable (summer 2026): International Terminal stop 84 → Sapporo Sta. 68–70 min;
  International → Odori Park 62–64 min; ANA Gate → Sapporo Sta. 74 min. 76 departures a day; first from the ANA stop 8:40,
  last 22:55 (International Terminal first 8:46, last 22:59).
- Express route (札幌都心直行便): two a day in the summer table (14:10 and 16:10 from the ANA stop), Sapporo Sta. 15:13, Odori Park, Susukino, Nakajima Park.
  Source: https://www.chuo-bus.co.jp/airport/timetable/?ope=det&n=35&o=2&t=14, fetched 24 Nov 2026. [bus_tt_35_2.html]
- Fare (do not put in body copy): Sapporo Sta. adult 1,500 yen, child 750 yen (same table).
- Payment (feature page, undated): "現金・クレジットカードのタッチ決済・往復券・回数券は、両社のバスでご利用いただけます。
  交通系ICカード（SAPICA・Kitaca・Suica等）は中央バス便のみ、PayPayは北都交通便のみご利用いただけます。"
  (Cash, contactless credit card, return and coupon tickets work on both companies' buses; **transport IC cards (SAPICA, Kitaca,
  Suica etc.) only on Chuo Bus buses**; PayPay only on Hokuto Kotsu buses.) The same page says "約 10 分間隔" (about every 10 minutes)
  and "予約なしでOK" (no reservation needed). Source: https://www.chuo-bus.co.jp/feature/airport/, fetched 24 Nov 2026. [bus_feature_airport.html]
- Rules (Chuo Bus "ご利用案内"): "天候・交通事情等により到着が遅れることがありますので、航空便との接続は余裕をもってご利用願います。
  航空便で遅延が発生した場合でも、当該バスは定刻で出発致します" (arrival may be late due to weather and traffic, so allow
  time for flight connections; buses leave on schedule even if flights are delayed). Big bags go in the trunk; tell the staff
  your stop. Full bus: you may be offered a fold-down seat or the next bus.
  Source: https://www.chuo-bus.co.jp/airport/beginner, fetched 24 Nov 2026. [bus_beginner.html]
- Bags accepted in the trunk: under 30 kg, under 0.25 m³, under 2 m long. Source: https://www.chuo-bus.co.jp/airport/baggage, fetched 24 Nov 2026.
- Sapporo Tourist Association: "Chuo Bus and Hokuto Kotsu Bus operate services from the airport to Sapporo City departing every
  10–15 minutes. The bus takes approximately 70 minutes"; boarding at "Chuo Bus counter (first floor, JAL arrival lobby)",
  "Hokuto Kotsu Bus counter (first floor, ANA arrival lobby)", international terminal "Transportation Information Counter (first floor lobby)".
  "Tickets can be purchased at the bus counter or from ticket vending machines and used for buses from both companies."
  Source: https://www.sapporo.travel/en/info/about/transportation/, fetched 24 Nov 2026.
- Ski-resort buses: Chuo Bus links "ニセコスキーバス (千歳～ニセコ、札幌～ニセコ)" at https://teikan.chuo-bus.co.jp/course/18958,
  but that host is **blocked**. Rusutsu and Furano: not found. See Gaps.

### 4. Facilities

### 5. Winter and disruption
- JR Hokkaido "When train services are disrupted" (New Chitose Airport ⇒ Sapporo): check boards and announcements ("Some
  information boards may be only in Japanese. Please use a translation tool."); "Please consider using other forms of transportation."
  Alternatives listed: Airport Bus "Approx. 70-90 min", boarding "New Chitose Airport (Domestic / International Terminal)";
  Taxi "Approx. 60 min". "Alternative transportation (such as buses) may become extremely crowded when trains are cancelled."
  "Travel time may increase or services may be suspended depending on weather and road conditions." Refunds: "If you have a
  paper ticket and cancel your trip, a full refund will be provided." "Proof of delay or cancellation: A certificate can be
  issued upon request at the station." Source: https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/english/travel/airport_disruption.html, fetched 24 Nov 2026. [jrh_travel_airport_disruption.html]
- JR live status pages (English): Sapporo area incl. Airport Train https://www3.jrhokkaido.co.jp/webunkou/area_spo_en.html
  (linked from https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/; not fetched, www3 host refused the sandbox).
- JR FAQ: "we cannot compensate for other transportation fares ... We do not refund or compensate for meals, or other
  transportation fares, lodging fees that incur due to late or suspended trains."
  Source: https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/english/faq/faq10.html, fetched 24 Nov 2026.
- Sapporo Tourist Association news (17 Dec 2025): "outdoor temperatures are very cold. We recommend wearing warm clothing and
  shoes with good traction for snowy roads". Source: https://www.sapporo.travel/en/news/important-information-for-travelers-to-sapporo/, fetched 24 Nov 2026.
- Airport's own winter / snow-delay / cancelled-flight guidance: **not reachable** (Gaps).

### Between the terminals (walkway)
- **3F of the connecting facility is partly closed until about June 2027.** Notice "Notice regarding facility renovations at
  New Chitose Airport" (posted 9 Nov 2026): "Due to construction work, a portion of the passageway on the 3rd floor of the
  connecting facility will be closed to traffic. Please note that during the construction period, it will not be possible to
  travel between domestic flights and International Terminal Building Terminal Building the 3rd floor." Period: "September 2,
  2026 (Wednesday) - End of June 2027 (tentative)". Source: https://www.hokkaido-airports.com/en/new-chitose/info/20261109-7624.html
  [cts/notice_renov.html]. The floor-guide PDF also marks the 3F Connecting Path "Under Renovation (Passage Closed)".
- So the route between terminals is the **2F Connecting Corridor** (floor PDF shows moving walkways along it, marked with
  arrows "To Domestic Terminal Building" / "To International Terminal Building"). The JR route PDF (2024) also uses the 2F walkway.
- "Conveyor Walkway: Equipment to facilitate those who have difficulty in movement is provided in connecting path."
  Source: https://www.hokkaido-airports.com/en/new-chitose/access/jr/
- Walking time and walkway length: **not stated** anywhere on the airport pages fetched (English and Japanese). Still a gap.
- Free Wi-Fi works "within the Domestic and International Terminal buildings, excluding connecting facilities" (FAQ).

## JR Hokkaido traveller guides (owner-supplied PDFs, 24 Nov 2026; same files on JR's site)
**Manner and user guide (English/Chinese)**, https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/pdf/e-cn-mannerguide.pdf
- Tickets: a basic fare ticket is always needed. Limited express trains also need a limited express ticket (reserved seats);
  "Passengers without a seat number will have to stand", though you can sit temporarily in empty seats (ordinary cars only).
  A local or rapid train's reserved seats need a reserved seat ticket; the Airport Train has five of six cars non-reserved, so the basic fare is enough.
- IC cards: "can be used only in limited areas of Hokkaido"; both the boarding and the alighting station must be inside the same IC card area.
  Q: IC card to Biei, Furano, Noboribetsu and Toya? A: "IC cards cannot be used", even if you board inside the area.
  Q: New Chitose Airport to Sapporo and Otaru? A: yes, non-reserved seating "with just your IC card". "Some IC cards cannot be used, such as SAPICA."
- Ticket gates: insert the ticket or tap the IC card; up to three tickets at once; with a rail pass insert only the pass.
  At stations without automatic gates, show your ticket to staff. Do not keep tickets in a smartphone case (magnetic malfunction).
- One-man-operated trains (one or two cars, no conductor): doors do not open automatically, press the button.
  Without a ticket, take a numbered ticket when boarding and pay the fare in cash to the driver (no credit cards, no IC cards). May vary by station and time.
- Luggage on board: length + width + height 250 cm or less (length up to 2 m), up to 30 kg each, up to 2 pieces; skis, snowboards and bicycles fully in their bags;
  large suitcases in the luggage storage area; same-day luggage delivery from New Chitose Airport to partnering hotels (conditions apply).
- Lost and found: "find chat", an English/Chinese AI chat to search for items lost on JR Hokkaido trains or in stations (register via the QR code in the guide).
- On the train: wait for passengers to get off first; phones on silent, no calls (limited expresses: between cars); priority seats; no sitting on the floor;
  no selfie sticks or phones pointed at the tracks; do not stand at the bottom of escalators.
**Accessibility**, https://www.jrhokkaido.co.jp/global/pdf/e-accessibility2025.pdf
- Wheelchair users: contact station staff (ramp); "especially" in winter, as platforms are slippery with snow. Many stations are unmanned or have no lift,
  and staff may be absent even at manned ones, so JR recommends requesting assistance in advance with its inquiry form (date, station, time, wheelchair,
  caregiver, help needed); replies 9:00 to 17:50 JST on weekdays (not over New Year).
- Assistance dogs travel free with a Temporary Certificate of Overseas Assistance Dog Users (apply beforehand).
- Mobility scooters: up to 70 cm wide and 120 cm long and high (with further conditions), driven slowly (2 km/h) following staff.
