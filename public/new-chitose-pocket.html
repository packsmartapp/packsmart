Which terminal are you in?

International flights: International Terminal. Domestic flights and JR trains: Domestic Terminal.

INTL

International Terminal

West side. 3F Departures. 2F Arrivals: exchange, Seven Bank ATM, SIM machines, tourist information, prayer room. 1F buses (stop 84) and taxis. 4F hotel

🚶2F Connecting Corridor (moving walkways). 3F passage closed to about end June 2027
DOM

Domestic Terminal

East side. 2F Departures and shops. 1F Arrivals, bus stops 14 and 22 (Sapporo), bus counters. 3F food. 4F onsen and observation deck (closed Dec to Mar)

🚆 JR: go down to B1F

⬇️Lifts and escalators down to B1F
B1F

JR New Chitose Airport Station

Ticket gates, JR Information Desk (8:30 to 19:00), ticket office, JR lockers. Platforms on B2F. Airport Train to Sapporo: fastest 33 min
