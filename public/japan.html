Offline pocket guides

Your pocket guides

A pocket guide is a short, big-print helper for one airport or station. Open it on your phone, save it to your home screen, and it works like an app, even with no signal.

  • Finds what you need fast. Toilets, lockers, food, exits and trains, a couple of taps away.
  • Big print, plain English. With a screen you can show station or airport staff in Japanese.
  • Works with no signal once it is saved, so it still helps in a basement or on a train platform.
  • Checked against official sources. The date it was last checked is at the top of every guide.

Save it in three taps

  1. Open a guide on your phone.
  2. Android: tap Install. iPhone: tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from your home screen whenever you need it.
