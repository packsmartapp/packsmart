📋 Visa & Documents for Indians

Indian citizens need a visa to enter Singapore. There is no visa on arrival. The standard tourist visa allows a stay of up to 30 days (a limited 96-hour transit facility exists; see the transit tip below).

DocumentDetails
PassportValid for at least 6 months from travel date
E-visa applicationApply through a visa agent authorised by the Singapore High Commission (Indians cannot apply to ICA directly). Government fee about US$22 (₹2,100) plus the agent's service charge of up to about US$14 (₹1,300). Check the current fee with your agent
SG Arrival CardFree and compulsory for every traveller, visa holders included. Submit it online (ICA website or MyICA app) within 3 days before you arrive. It is not a visa
Return/onward ticketMandatory — immigration will check
Hotel bookingConfirmed accommodation for the entire stay
Proof of fundsBank statement (last 3 months) showing sufficient balance
Cover letterBrief letter stating purpose of visit, dates, and itinerary
💡 Pro Tip: Singapore visa processing takes 3–5 working days. Apply through an agent authorised by the Singapore High Commission in New Delhi (the list is on its website). The visa is electronically linked to your passport — no sticker required. Just carry a printout of the approval email.
⚠️ Transit Tip: If you're transiting through Changi Airport to another country, Indians can use the 96-hour Visa-Free Transit Facility (VFTF) only if they hold a valid visa or long-term pass (valid for at least 1 month) for the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Japan, Germany or Switzerland, and have an onward air or sea ticket leaving within 96 hours. Without one of those, you need a Singapore visa.

🌦️ Singapore Weather: What to Expect

Singapore sits almost exactly on the equator, so the weather is hot and humid year-round. There are no real "seasons" — just slightly wetter months (Nov–Jan) and slightly drier months (Feb–Sep). Pack for heat and sudden rain every single day.

👚 The Complete Singapore Packing List

Clothing

  • 5–6 breathable tops — Cotton or moisture-wicking fabric. You will sweat the moment you step outdoors
  • 2–3 shorts — Perfectly acceptable everywhere (Singapore is casual)
  • 1–2 light pants — For nicer restaurants and air-conditioned comfort
  • 1 light jacket or cardigan — Absolute must. Singapore's AC is Arctic-level in malls, MRT, restaurants, and attractions
  • 1 swimsuit — For Sentosa beaches, hotel pools, and Adventure Cove
  • Comfortable walking shoes — Waterproof or quick-dry preferred. You'll walk 15,000+ steps daily
  • Flip-flops — For Sentosa, beach areas, and hostel use
  • 1 nice outfit — For Marina Bay Sands area, Clarke Quay nightlife, or rooftop bars
💡 Packing Hack: Pack a compact foldable umbrella or poncho in your day bag — every single day. Rain in Singapore hits suddenly, lasts 30–60 minutes, then clears. If you're caught without one, you'll be stuck under a shelter for an hour.

Toiletries & Health

  • Sunscreen SPF 50 — The equatorial sun is deceptively strong even on cloudy days
  • Mosquito repellent patches/cream — Singapore battles dengue actively, especially in garden areas
  • Basic medicines — Paracetamol, antacids, ORS. Pharmacies (Guardian, Watsons) are on every corner but are pricier than India
  • Hand sanitizer
  • Deodorant — Non-negotiable in this humidity

Electronics & Tech

  • Power adapter — Singapore uses Type G plugs (UK-style, 3 rectangular pins). Same as Dubai/UK. Indian plugs won't fit
  • Power bank — Essential for all-day outings. Can also rent portable chargers at MRT stations via ChargeSpot
  • EZ-Link or SimplyGo card — Buy at Changi or any MRT station for public transport. Or use contactless Visa/Mastercard

⚠️ Singapore's Strict Laws (Seriously, Read This)

Singapore is famously strict about public behaviour. These aren't suggestions — they're enforced with heavy fines:

  • No chewing gum — Importing, selling, or chewing gum is banned (medical gum excepted). Fine: SGD 1,000+
  • No littering — First offense: SGD 300 fine. Repeat offenders: Corrective Work Order (public cleaning)
  • No smoking in public — Only in designated yellow-box smoking areas. Fine: SGD 200–1,000
  • No jaywalking — Cross only at zebra crossings and traffic lights. Fine: SGD 50
  • No eating/drinking on MRT — Fine: SGD 500. Even water is not allowed on the train
  • No drugs — Singapore has the death penalty for drug trafficking. Possession of even small quantities carries severe punishment. Zero tolerance
  • No durian on MRT — Yes, they're serious about this. The fruit is banned from public transport and most hotels
⚠️ Vape Warning: E-cigarettes and vapes are completely banned in Singapore. Importing, using, or possessing vapes can result in fines up to SGD 2,000 for first offense. Leave your vape at home.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Indians need a visa for Singapore?
Yes, Indians need a tourist visa for Singapore, and there is no visa on arrival. You cannot apply to ICA directly: the application goes through a visa agent authorised by the Singapore High Commission. The government fee is about US$22 (₹2,100) plus the agent's service charge (up to about US$14, or ₹1,300), the visa typically allows a 30-day stay, and processing takes 3-5 working days. You also need a passport valid for at least 6 months and must submit the free SG Arrival Card online within 3 days before you land. Check the current fee with your agent.
What should I pack for Singapore from India?
Pack light, breathable clothes — Singapore is hot and humid year-round (25-33°C). Bring a light jacket for air-conditioned spaces, comfortable walking shoes, an umbrella or rain jacket (rain can come suddenly), a universal power adapter (Type G outlets), and smart-casual clothes for restaurants and attractions.
What is the best time to visit Singapore from India?
Singapore can be visited year-round as it's tropical. February to April tends to be driest. June-August (Great Singapore Sale) is popular for shopping. November-January sees more rain but also Christmas and New Year celebrations. Avoid Chinese New Year if you want cheaper hotels. Direct flights from India take 5-6 hours.
How much does a Singapore trip cost from India?
A 5-day Singapore trip costs approximately ₹50,000-80,000 per person on a budget, ₹80,000-1,50,000 for mid-range, and ₹1,50,000+ for luxury. Return flights from India cost ₹15,000-30,000. Singapore is pricier than Southeast Asian neighbors but has excellent public transport (MRT) and affordable hawker centre food (₹200-400/meal).
