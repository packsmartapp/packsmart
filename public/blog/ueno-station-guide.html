Ueno Station is where many visitors first meet Tokyo: the Keisei Skyliner from Narita Airport arrives at Keisei Ueno, Keisei’s own station, and Ueno Park, with its museums and cherry trees, is a short walk from the JR gates. It also has a JR line called the “Narita Line” that does not go to the airport. This guide is written in plain English so that a first-time visitor, a family or an older traveller can follow it step by step. You can print the one-page card below and carry it with you.

Ueno Station on one page

  • To Narita Airport: the Keisei Skyliner from Keisei Ueno, a separate station. Keisei gives 41 minutes from Narita Airport Terminal 2·3 to Keisei Ueno.
  • Not JR's Narita Line: tracks 10 to 12 are “for Matsudo, Toride, Mito & Narita”. That is Narita city, not the airport.
  • Ueno Park: the Park Gate or the Panda Bridge Exit, both on 3F. About a 2-minute walk (park office). Open 5:00 to 23:00.
  • Main side: Central Gate and Main Exit on 1F, with the taxi stop, the ticket office and the JR EAST Travel Service Center.
  • JR platforms: tracks 1 to 12 on 2F (Yamanote Line 2 and 3), 13 to 17 on 1F, Shinkansen 19 to 22 underground.
  • Tokyo Metro: Ginza Line G16 and Hibiya Line H18. Follow the “for Tokyo Metro” signs near the Central Gate.
  • Lockers: on 1F, 2F and 3F (JR East map). Lost & Found: 2F, by the Panda Bridge Exit stairs.
  • Need help? Question & Answer counter beside the Central Gate (1F). In an emergency, call 119.

To Narita Airport: the Skyliner, not JR's Narita Line

⚠️ JR's “Narita Line” does not mean Narita Airport

JR East's station map lists the Narita Line on tracks 10 to 12 (2F) “for Matsudo, Toride, Mito & Narita”, and also on tracks 6 and 9. That Narita is the city. Do not board it for your flight.

For Narita Airport, take the Keisei Skyliner from Keisei Ueno, Keisei's own station, separate from JR Ueno.

  • How long. Keisei gives 41 minutes on the Skyliner from Narita Airport Terminal 2·3 to Keisei Ueno. Its Skyliner page also says “in as little as 41 minutes” from Narita Airport to Keisei Ueno; the “as little as 36 minutes” figure on the same page is to Nippori.
  • Where to board. At Keisei Ueno, not in the JR station. We did not find an official walking route between JR Ueno and Keisei Ueno, so follow the signs or ask station staff. Keisei links its station maps for Keisei Ueno and Nippori from its station page.
  • Tickets. Keisei sells a Skyliner discount ticket between Narita Airport Station and Ueno Station, and a Skyliner and Tokyo Subway Ticket set. Fares are on Keisei's own pages, and they change. You can also book Skyliner tickets online (partner link); before you pay, read the listing's change and cancellation rules.
  • Holding the “Skyliner & Tokyo Subway Ticket” or the “Greater Tokyo Pass”? Keisei says you cannot transfer at Nippori with these, so use Ueno.
  • Changing to JR at Nippori? Keisei says the JR transfer at Nippori needs a JR ticket.
  • First and last Skyliner. We did not find them on the pages we read. Check Keisei's timetable before a very early or late trip.

Tokyo Metro: Ginza and Hibiya lines

  • Lines. Ginza Line (G16) and Hibiya Line (H18). Tokyo Metro lists transfers to JR East and to Keisei.
  • From the JR station. JR East's map shows “for Tokyo Metro” signs near the Central Gate (1F), and on 2F a route to the Ginza and Hibiya lines.
  • Exits. We did not find Tokyo Metro's exit list for Ueno. For Ueno Park, the park office gives a 4-minute walk from the Ginza and Hibiya line station. If you are unsure, go up into the JR station and use the Park Gate or Panda Bridge on 3F.
  • Tickets. Tokyo Metro says its multi-functional ticket machines are at all ticket offices, from the first train to the last. Ueno also has a Tokyo Metro Pass Office (for commuter passes; the map we saw does not say whether it sells visitor tickets).
  • Lost something on the Metro? On the same day, ask the station office where you lost it. From the next day, contact the Lost and Found at Iidabashi Station (Namboku Line) or the Tokyo Metro Customer Relations Center.
  • Station office. 03-3832-6629. Address: 3-19-6 Higashiueno, Taito-ku, Tokyo.
  • Busy. Tokyo Metro counts 191,804 passengers a day on average in 2025 at Ueno (10th of its 130 stations), not counting passengers on directly connected lines.

Routes change, so follow the signs.

Ueno Park and the cherry blossoms

Ueno Park (Ueno Onshi Park) is a short walk from the station. The Tokyo Metropolitan Government calls it one of Japan's best-known cherry blossom spots, and the park association describes it as known for cherry blossoms and Shinobazu Pond. It opened in 1873 and was one of Japan's first designated parks.

  • Walking times (park office). JR Ueno (Yamanote, Keihin-Tohoku, Takasaki and Utsunomiya lines): 2 minutes. Tokyo Metro Ginza and Hibiya lines, Ueno: 4 minutes. Toei Oedo Line, Ueno-okachimachi: 5 minutes. Keisei Main Line, Keisei Ueno: 1 minute.
  • From JR Ueno. Use the Park Gate or the Panda Bridge Exit on 3F.
  • Opening hours. 5:00 to 23:00. There is no entry outside these hours.
  • Cherry trees. About 1,200, among the park's main plants.
  • When. The park office says the blossoms last for about two months, from the early-flowering kanzakura to the Somei-yoshino. Dates change every year, so check the official cherry blossom forecasts nearer the time before you plan a day around them.
  • Summer. The lotus on Shinobazu Pond is at its best in late July.
  • Park rules. The park's rules page lists no fireworks and no catch-ball, among others.
  • Nearby. Keisei lists Ueno Park, Ueno Zoo, the Tokyo National Museum, Kaneiji Temple, the National Science Museum, Shinobazu Pond and Ameyoko near Keisei Ueno. Check each one's own opening hours.

Hours and rules can change, so check the park's pages.

Lockers and luggage

Sort your bags out before you head into the park or the museums. JR East's station map shows coin lockers on three floors.

  • 1F. Near the Main Exit, on the taxi side, and inside the ticket gates by the Transfer to Shinkansen gate.
  • 2F. Along the concourse to the Iriya Exit.
  • 3F. By the Panda Bridge Exit and near the Iriya Gate, on the park side.
  • Sizes and prices. We did not find them on the official pages, so we give none.
  • Keep with you: medicines, valuables, chargers and your passport.
  • Have these ready: your hotel name, address and phone number, written down.

Help for older travellers and anyone who needs assistance

You do not have to cope alone. Asking early is easier than struggling.

  • Question & Answer counter. JR East's map puts it on 1F, beside the Central Gate.
  • JR EAST Travel Service Center and ticket office. On 1F, by the Central Gate ticket hall.
  • Lifts. JR East's map letters them A to I: A to F go from 3F to platforms 1 to 12, G and H to the Shinkansen platforms, and I to the 2F concourse outside the ticket gates.
  • Station staff. Tell staff at a ticket gate if you need help or are not sure which way to go.
  • Tokyo Metro. The Metro station office number is 03-3832-6629 (Tokyo Metro).
  • Allow extra time. We did not find official walking times between gates and platforms, so add a buffer, especially with luggage.
  • Visitor hotline. The Japan Visitor Hotline, 050-3816-2787, is open 24 hours and helps in English, Chinese and Korean. In an emergency, call 119.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get from Ueno to Narita Airport?

Take the Keisei Skyliner from Keisei Ueno station, which is Keisei's own station, separate from JR Ueno. Keisei gives 41 minutes from Narita Airport Terminal 2·3 to Keisei Ueno on the Skyliner. We did not find the first and last Skyliner times on the pages we read, so check Keisei's timetable before you travel.

Does JR's Narita Line from Ueno go to Narita Airport?

Do not rely on it for the airport. JR East's station map lists the Narita Line on tracks 10 to 12 "for Matsudo, Toride, Mito & Narita": that Narita is the city, not the airport. For Narita Airport, use the Keisei Skyliner from Keisei Ueno.

Is Keisei Ueno the same station as JR Ueno?

No. Keisei Ueno is Keisei's own station. We did not find an official walking route between JR Ueno and Keisei Ueno, so follow the signs or ask station staff. Tokyo Metro lists Keisei as a transfer at Ueno, and the Ueno Park office says Keisei Ueno is 1 minute's walk from the park.

Can I change from the Skyliner to the JR lines at Nippori instead?

Keisei says that with the "Skyliner & Tokyo Subway Ticket" or the "Greater Tokyo Pass" you cannot transfer at Nippori, so use Ueno. Changing to JR at Nippori also needs a JR ticket.

Which exit do I use for Ueno Park?

Use the Park Gate or the Panda Bridge Exit, both on the 3rd floor. JR East's map marks the end of Panda Bridge "for Ueno Park". The park office gives a 2-minute walk from JR Ueno.

When are the cherry blossoms in Ueno Park?

The park office says Ueno Park has about 1,200 cherry trees and that the blossoms last about two months, from the early-flowering kanzakura to the Somei-yoshino. The dates change every year, so check the official cherry blossom forecasts nearer the time. The park is open from 5:00 to 23:00, with no entry outside those hours.

Which platform is the Yamanote Line at Ueno?

JR East's station map shows track 2 for Tabata, Ikebukuro and Shinjuku, and track 3 for Akihabara, Tokyo, Hamamatsucho and Shinagawa. Both are on the 2nd floor.

Where are the Shinkansen platforms at Ueno?

Underground, on tracks 19 to 22 (floors B4 to B1). Tracks 19 and 20 are for the Tohoku, Yamagata, Akita, Hokkaido, Joetsu and Hokuriku Shinkansen, and tracks 21 and 22 for Tokyo. JR East's map shows a Transfer to Shinkansen gate inside the station on 1F, and lifts G and H to the Shinkansen platforms.

Where can I leave my luggage at Ueno Station?

JR East's station map shows coin lockers on 1F (near the Main Exit and taxi side, and inside the gates by the Shinkansen transfer), on 2F (along the concourse to the Iriya Exit) and on 3F (by the Panda Bridge Exit and near the Iriya Gate). We did not find their sizes or prices.

Where is the lost and found at Ueno Station?

JR East's map puts Lost & Found on 2F, near the stairs to the Panda Bridge Exit. For something lost on the Tokyo Metro, Tokyo Metro says: on the same day, ask the station office where you lost it; from the next day, contact the Lost and Found at Iidabashi Station or the Tokyo Metro Customer Relations Center.

Which Tokyo Metro lines stop at Ueno, and which exit should I use?

The Ginza Line (G16) and the Hibiya Line (H18). We did not find Tokyo Metro's exit list, so this guide routes you by the JR gates: for Ueno Park, the Park Gate or Panda Bridge on 3F. The park office gives a 4-minute walk to the park from the Metro station.
