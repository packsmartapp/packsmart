Sapporo Station is where most visitors to Hokkaido’s biggest city arrive: the Airport Train from New Chitose stops here, the subway runs on to Odori Park and Susukino, and the limited expresses leave for Hakodate and Asahikawa. It is also a building site, being rebuilt for the Hokkaido Shinkansen, so routes inside it change. This guide is written in plain English so that a first-time visitor, a family or an older traveller can follow it step by step. You can print the one-page card below and carry it with you.

Sapporo Station on one page

  • Under construction: JR is rebuilding parts of the station for the Shinkansen. Its map is dated 21 October 2026 and may change. Some lifts and escalators by the East gate are out of use.
  • Two ticket gates: the West gate and the East gate. North Exit plaza and South Exit plaza (taxis).
  • Subway: Namboku Line N06 (west side) to Odori in 2 minutes and Susukino in 3. Toho Line H07 (east side).
  • Help: JR Information Desk at the West Exit (8:30 to 19:00). Tourist Information Center in the West Concourse (8:30 to 20:00).
  • Lockers: about 800, plus 5 for skis and snowboards. Up to 3 days.
  • To New Chitose Airport: JR's Airport Train, fastest 33 minutes. Check the boards for your platform.
  • Snow Festival 2027: 4 to 11 February at Odori Park, Tsudome and Susukino.
  • Shinkansen: not in Sapporo yet. For Hakodate, take the Limited Express Hokuto.

Under construction: what it means for you

🚧 Sapporo Station is being rebuilt for the Hokkaido Shinkansen

JR Hokkaido is working on the station for the Hokkaido Shinkansen extension to Sapporo. Its station search page does not show a normal map of Sapporo: it sends you to the construction page, whose station map is dated 21 October 2026 and “may change as work progresses”.

What the map shows: several areas under construction (some still passable), and some escalators and lifts by the East gate out of use, with the stairs usable.

  • Follow the signs on the day. Walkways move as the work goes on, so a route from an old map or video may be closed.
  • Need step-free access? Ask station staff before you set off, especially on the East gate side.
  • Allow extra time. We did not find official walking times inside the station, so add a buffer, especially with luggage or skis.

The map may change as the work goes on.

Gates and exits: West and East

JR's station map (as of 21 October 2026) shows two ticket gates and two exit plazas. Platform stairs 2 to 11 are inside the gates. The subway is underground, with the Namboku Line towards the west and the Toho Line towards the east.

Information, tickets and tourist help

  • JR Information Desk (for visitors). At the JR Sapporo Station West Exit. JR says it helps in English, Chinese and Korean (not every language is always available), exchanges rail pass vouchers, and sells rail passes and train tickets. Hours on JR's map: 8:30 to 19:00 (the map is undated, so check).
  • JR ticket office. 5:40 to 22:00. Reserved-seat and talking ticket machines 5:30 to 23:50 (with an operator 6:00 to 23:00).
  • Hokkaido-Sapporo Tourist Information Center. In the Hokkaido-Sapporo “Food and Tourism” Information Building, JR Sapporo West Concourse (Kita 6-jo Nishi 4-chome). 8:30 to 20:00, open all year, in Japanese, English, Chinese and Korean.
  • Station staff. Tell staff at a ticket gate if you need help or are not sure which way to go. In an emergency, call 119.
  • Busy station. The Sapporo Tourist Association says about 90,000 people a day use JR Sapporo Station, JR Hokkaido's largest hub.

Hours can change.

Subway: Namboku and Toho lines, to Odori and Susukino

The subway station under JR Sapporo is called Sapporo too, but the city writes it in hiragana, さっぽろ, while JR writes 札幌. Two lines stop here: the Namboku Line (N06) and the Toho Line (H07).

  • To Odori (Snow Festival main site). Namboku Line to Odori (N07): 2 minutes.
  • To Susukino. Namboku Line to Susukino (N08): 3 minutes. The Toho Line has a station called Hosui-Susukino (H09).
  • First and last trains towards Odori. On the timetable dated 28 February 2025, trains towards Makomanai (via Odori and Susukino) leave Sapporo from 6:09 to 0:09, on weekdays and at weekends. Towards Asabu: 6:18 to 0:18. Check the city's current timetable before a late night out.
  • Changing between Namboku and Toho. You have to go out through the gates and come back in within 30 minutes. With a paper ticket, use the yellow transfer gates (three each at the Namboku and Toho south gates). Go in through the gate of the line you plan to ride: if you enter at the Namboku gates, you cannot leave towards the Toho Line.
  • Exits. The city's map shows Exits 2 and 18 leading to JR Sapporo Station, and Exit 11 into the Sapporo Ekimae-dori underground walkway, which runs towards Odori. We did not find where the walkway comes out, its length or its hours. Exits 4 and 5 have been closed since 3 August 2024.
  • Odori has three stations: N07 on the Namboku Line, H08 on the Toho Line and T09 on the Tozai Line.

Timetables change, so check the city's pages.

Sapporo Snow Festival 2027

The festival's official Japanese site gives the 2027 dates as Thursday 4 February to Thursday 11 February 2027 (11 February is a national holiday) at all three main sites. Its English page still shows older years, so go by the Japanese page.

  • Odori Park site. Odori Park from Nishi 1-chome to Nishi 11-chome: the big snow sculptures and projection mapping. Subway Namboku Line from Sapporo to Odori, 2 minutes.
  • Susukino site. Ice sculptures. Namboku Line from Sapporo to Susukino, 3 minutes.
  • Tsudome site. Slides, snow rafts and indoor food booths. We did not find the way there on the pages we read, so check the festival's site.
  • Premist Dome site. Listed on the festival's site, with no 2027 dates yet.
  • Opening hours and lighting times. Not on the festival's top page yet. Check nearer the time.
  • Dress for it. The Sapporo Tourist Association says it is very cold outside and recommends warm clothing and shoes with good grip for snowy roads.

Dates and sites can change, so check the festival's site.

To New Chitose Airport: the Airport Train

JR Hokkaido's Airport Train (the Rapid Airport) runs directly between Sapporo (01) and New Chitose Airport (AP15), and some trains start or end at Otaru.

  • How fast. JR gives the fastest times between the airport and Sapporo as 33 minutes (Special Rapid), 37 minutes (Rapid) and 43 minutes (Semi-Rapid). On the way, every type stops at Shin-Sapporo and Minami-Chitose.
  • How often. JR's page gives six trains an hour between 9:00 and 16:00 from the airport to Sapporo. It does not give the times the other way, so check the timetable.
  • Which platform? We did not find a current official platform list for the Airport Train at Sapporo, so check the departure boards.
  • First and last trains. JR Hokkaido’s timetable for 24 November 2026 (a weekday): from New Chitose Airport, the first train to Sapporo is the Rapid Airport at 6:38 (Sapporo 7:18), and the last is the Special Rapid Airport at 23:21 (Sapporo 23:59). From Sapporo to the airport, the first is the Rapid Airport at 5:50 (airport 6:28), and the last is a local train at 22:25, which stops everywhere and reaches the airport at 23:12. Timetables change, so check JR’s departures the day before.
  • Rail passes for visitors. JR Hokkaido’s Sapporo–Noboribetsu Area Pass and Sapporo–Furano Area Pass (4 consecutive days each) include New Chitose Airport, and the Hokkaido Rail Pass (5, 7 or 10 days) covers all JR Hokkaido lines except the Hokkaido Shinkansen. JR says all non-Japanese passport holders can use them; show your passport itself when you buy or exchange one (a copy is not accepted). With a pass you can sit in the reserved car or the non-reserved cars. Put only the pass into the ticket gate and take it back: JR does not reissue a lost pass. The passes do not cover the subway.
  • Seats. Cars 1 to 3 and 5 to 6 are non-reserved. Car 4 is the reserved “U seat” car: you need a reserved-seat ticket as well as the fare.
  • IC cards work (Suica, Kitaca and others). Ticket machines take cash only; the reserved-seat machine and the ticket office take cash and credit cards.
  • Luggage. Within 250 cm in total (2 m at most in length), up to 2 items, each up to 30 kg.
  • Passport-reader ticket machines. JR has posted a notice (8 October 2026) with important notes on its ticket machines with passport readers at Sapporo and New Chitose Airport stations. We did not read it, so check it on JR’s English home page.
  • Live train information. Check JR Hokkaido’s Train Operation Information (English), or its information for each station, which also shows that day’s departures. JR’s guide to it describes delays of 5 minutes or more by station (5:00 to 25:00), a map of cancellations and delays of 30 minutes or more by line, including the Chitose Line (Sapporo, New Chitose Airport, Tomakomai), 5:00 to 24:00, and train locations with delays of 1 minute or more (5:00 to 25:00).
  • By bus instead. From 11 December 2026 the airport bus leaves the Sapporo Station stop (Sapporo Ekimae) from 5:25 to 18:15, 68 a day, and takes about 77 minutes. No booking needed. The bus companies show the stop on their route map. Until 10 December the summer timetable applies.
  • At the airport. The train arrives at B1F under the Domestic Terminal. For an international flight, go up to 2F and walk the 2F corridor: the 3F passage is closed until about the end of June 2027.

More detail: our New Chitose Airport guide covers the terminals, the corridor, buses, money and what to do in snow.

Times change, so check JR Hokkaido.

Hakodate, Asahikawa, Otaru and the Shinkansen

  • The Shinkansen does not reach Sapporo yet. JR Hokkaido's construction page describes its work as towards the opening of the Hokkaido Shinkansen's Sapporo station, and JR lists the Hokkaido Shinkansen stations as Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto, Kikonai and Okutsugaru-Imabetsu. We did not find an opening date on those pages.
  • To Hakodate and the Shinkansen. JR: the Limited Express Hokuto connects Sapporo and Hakodate, and is used as a connecting train to and from the Hokkaido Shinkansen. The Sapporo Tourist Association gives about 3 hours 30 minutes to Hakodate. Staying there? JR says JR Inn Hakodate is right by JR Hakodate Station. Rooms and prices (partner link)
  • Book a seat on limited expresses. JR Hokkaido: “From March 14, 2026, all seats will be reserved on all limited express trains in Hokkaido.” That includes the Hokuto, Kamui, Lilac and Ozora. The Airport Train is not a limited express: it keeps its non-reserved cars.
  • To Asahikawa. The Limited Express Kamui and Lilac. The Sapporo Tourist Association gives 1 hour 25 minutes at the quickest, with over 20 round trips a day.
  • To Kushiro. The Limited Express Ozora, about 4 hours 11 minutes (Sapporo Tourist Association).
  • To Otaru. Some Airport Trains run on to Otaru. JR says trains bound for “Teine” or “Hoshimi” do not continue to Otaru.
  • Going beyond Otaru to Yoichi, Kutchan or Niseko? JR says IC cards such as Suica cannot be used beyond Otaru: buy a ticket at the station before you board. JR’s English home page also notes that IC cards work only at some stations.

Times change, so check JR Hokkaido.

Day trips by train, and ski buses

Sapporo is a good base: Otaru, Noboribetsu and Asahikawa are all easy day trips by train. Train times below are from JR Hokkaido’s timetables for Tuesday 24 November 2026 (a weekday). They change, so check JR’s departures the day before.

Before you go: four JR rules

  • Limited expresses: book a seat. All seats on all limited expresses in Hokkaido are reserved: you need a reserved-seat ticket as well as the fare, and JR says passengers without a seat number may have to stand. With a rail pass, get your seat ticket before you board: seats cannot be given on the train.
  • IC cards work only near Sapporo. Suica, Kitaca and the other IC cards work to Otaru and the airport. JR says they cannot be used to Noboribetsu, Biei, Furano or Toya, even if you board inside the IC area, nor beyond Otaru to Kutchan and Niseko. Buy a paper ticket. JR also says some IC cards, such as SAPICA, cannot be used on its trains.
  • One-man trains (one or two cars, no conductor, as on the local lines to Biei and Furano): the doors do not open by themselves, so press the button. With no ticket, take a numbered ticket when you board and pay the driver in cash: no credit cards, no IC cards.
  • Tickets and bags. Do not keep paper tickets in a phone case: JR warns they can stop working. Skis and snowboards must be fully in their bags. Each bag within 250 cm in total (2 m long at most), up to 30 kg, and up to 2 bags.

Otaru

  • Train. The Rapid and Special Rapid Airport run through to Otaru about every 30 minutes in the daytime, 34 to 46 minutes. The last Rapid Airport leaves Sapporo at 22:53, and local trains run until 23:50. Back from Otaru, Rapid trains run until 19:56, then local trains.
  • IC cards work to Otaru, and the Airport Train’s non-reserved cars need no seat ticket.
  • Winter. JR lists the Otaru Snow Light Path in early to mid February.
  • If trains stop, JR lists a bus between Sapporo and Otaru, about 60 to 80 minutes.

Noboribetsu hot springs (and Upopoy on the way)

  • Train. Limited Express Suzuran or Hokuto to Noboribetsu Station, 70 to 82 minutes. First 6:53, last 21:12. Back: the last limited express leaves Noboribetsu at 21:21. The 6:00 Hokuto does not stop at Noboribetsu.
  • To the hot-spring town. Noboribetsu Onsen is about 8 km from the station (JNTO). Donan Bus runs a bus from the station, 27 minutes. IC cards cannot be used on it. On its weekday timetable the first bus leaves the station at 6:52 and the last at 20:04; back from Noboribetsu Onsen, first 6:40, last 20:35.
  • Upopoy (National Ainu Museum and Park): get off at Shiraoi, about 1 hour from Sapporo; it is about a 10-minute walk from the North Exit. Not every limited express stops at Shiraoi.
  • No IC cards to Noboribetsu: buy a paper ticket.

Asahikawa and Asahiyama Zoo

  • Train. Limited Express Lilac or Kamui, 85 minutes. First 6:29, last 22:00; back, last 22:00 too. From 1 December 2026 JR sells e-tickets for these two trains: you tap your IC card at the gate.
  • To the zoo. Asahikawa Denki Kido bus routes 41 and 47, from stop 6 in front of Asahikawa Station: about every 30 minutes, about 40 minutes.
  • Winter opening. 11 November 2026 to 7 April 2027, 10:30 to 15:30 (last entry 15:00). Closed 30 December to 1 January.
  • Penguin walk. In the snowy season, from about late December to mid-March, at 11:00 and 14:30 (March: 11:00 only). The zoo announces the first day on its social media, and the walk is called off in bad weather. The paths are steep: wear shoes that grip on snow.
  • Staying the night? JR says JR Inn Asahikawa is directly connected to Asahikawa Station. Rooms and prices (partner link)

Biei and Furano in winter

  • No direct train in winter. The Furano Lavender Express runs June to September only. JR’s winter route goes via Asahikawa, then local trains: about 35 minutes to Biei, and 66 to 83 minutes from Asahikawa to Furano.
  • Few trains. On 24 November there were up to 2 hours between local trains in the morning (Biei to Furano: none between 10:14 and 12:09). Plan your way back before you go.
  • Farm Tomita in winter: from Naka-Furano Station, about 25 minutes on foot (JR). JR marks its Blue Pond trip as one for the season without snow.
  • No IC cards on these lines, and the trains are one-man: see the rules above.

Niseko: take the ski bus

  • Why not the train? No direct daytime train: you change at Otaru, often at Kutchan too, and IC cards do not work.
  • Ski bus (Hokkaido Chuo Bus with Niseko Bus), booking required, 12 December 2026 to 22 March 2027 (the first and last days depend on the snow). From the Sapporo Station North Exit at 7:50 and 9:20, at Niseko Hirafu (Welcome Center) at 10:56 and 12:26: about 3 hours. Back from Hirafu at 14:39 and 15:59, at Sapporo Station at 17:45 and 19:05.
  • How to book. Online in English, or by phone at the Chuo Bus Sapporo Terminal, 0570-200-600 (7:30 to 18:00), by 18:00 the day before. Seats are not numbered. Online bookings are not paid online: buy the ticket at a counter on the day, or pay cash on the bus.
  • Ski gear. No ski or snowboard boots on board. Put skis and snowboards in a case (sole guards are fine for snowboards).
  • Closer slopes. A booked bus runs from the Sapporo Station North Exit (taxi-pool side) to the Sapporo Kokusai ski area, about 1 hour 37 minutes, from the ski area’s opening (from 20 November 2026) to 5 May 2027. It does not take you if it is full. Check its winter times on the bus company’s page.

If something goes wrong

  • Lost something on a JR train? JR Hokkaido’s “find chat” is an English and Chinese chat for items lost on its trains or in its stations; the QR code is in JR’s manner guide.
  • Need help with a wheelchair? Many stations have no staff or no lift, so JR recommends asking for help in advance with its inquiry form. Platforms are slippery with snow in winter.
  • Snow. Check JR Hokkaido’s Train Operation Information (English) before you set off, and keep a little cash for a bus or a taxi.

Money and ATMs

Seven Bank ATMs take cards issued outside Japan with the Visa, Mastercard, UnionPay, American Express, JCB, Discover and Diners Club marks, though Seven Bank says some cards with these marks may not be accepted. These are the Seven Bank ATMs its locator lists at Sapporo Station:

  • JR Sapporo Station, West gate side: 1 ATM, 5:10 to 24:00.
  • Subway, Namboku Line Sapporo (N06): 2 ATMs in the concourse outside the ticket gates, 6:00 to 24:00.
  • Subway, Toho Line Sapporo (H07): 1 ATM on B1, in the gate concourse near the South gate, 6:00 to 24:00.
  • JR Tower APIA: 1 ATM in the cash service corner (Floral Galleria), 8:00 to 21:00.
  • At the Snow Festival: Odori subway station has 2 ATMs outside the ticket gates, 6:00 to 24:00: on B2 by the East gate (Toho Line) and on B1 by exit 4 (Tozai Line).
  • Currency exchange: we did not find a counter at the station on the official pages we read.

Seven Bank says the hours can differ on the day, so carry a little cash before a late train.

Lockers and luggage

Sort your bags out before you head to Odori or the ski slopes.

  • How many. JR's locker list (July 2026) gives about 800 lockers at Sapporo, in small, medium and large sizes. JR says the number goes up and down with the construction.
  • Skis and snowboards. Sapporo has 5 coin lockers for skis and snowboards; JR says its other stations have none.
  • How long. Up to 3 days, counting the day you start.
  • Two kinds. The Sapporo Tourist Association describes key lockers, which take only 100-yen coins, and touch-panel lockers with English, simplified Chinese and Korean. It says the station lockers can be opened from 5:15 to midnight.
  • Where and how much. We did not find a locker map or a current price list in English, so we give none. JR's English FAQ says locker information is in Japanese only. Follow the signs.
  • Keep with you: medicines, valuables, chargers and your passport.

Locker numbers change with the construction.

Leaving Sapporo: a checklist

  • Flying from New Chitose? Take JR's Airport Train (fastest 33 minutes). The first leaves at 5:50 and the last at 22:25 (a local train, at the airport 23:12), on JR’s timetable for 24 November 2026. Check the departure boards for the platform, and JR’s departures the day before an early flight. See our New Chitose Airport guide.
  • International flight? At the airport, walk the 2F corridor from the Domestic Terminal; the 3F passage is closed until about the end of June 2027.
  • Snow forecast? Leave earlier, and check JR Hokkaido’s Train Operation Information (English). If trains stop, JR lists the airport bus and taxis as alternatives and warns they get very crowded.
  • Heading to Hakodate or the Shinkansen? The Limited Express Hokuto. Since 14 March 2026 all seats on all limited expresses in Hokkaido are reserved (JR), so book a seat.
  • Collect your bags. Lockers hold bags for up to 3 days, and the station is under construction, so leave time to find your locker again.
  • Allow time for the station itself. Routes change with the works, and some lifts and escalators by the East gate are out of use.

Times change, so check the operator.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get from Sapporo Station to Odori Park?

Take the subway Namboku Line from Sapporo (N06) one stop to Odori (N07): the city gives 2 minutes. The subway map also shows Exit 11 leading into the Sapporo Ekimae-dori underground walkway, which runs towards Odori; we did not find where it comes out, its length or its hours.

How do I get from Sapporo Station to Susukino?

The Namboku Line from Sapporo (N06) to Susukino (N08) takes 3 minutes, according to the Sapporo City Transportation Bureau.

When is the Sapporo Snow Festival 2027?

The festival's official Japanese site gives Thursday 4 February to Thursday 11 February 2027 (a national holiday) at all three main sites: Odori Park, Tsudome and Susukino. It also lists a Premist Dome site, with no 2027 dates yet. Opening and lighting times were not on the page.

Is Sapporo Station under construction?

Yes. JR Hokkaido is rebuilding parts of the station for the Hokkaido Shinkansen extension to Sapporo. Its station map is dated 21 October 2026 and JR says it may change as work goes on. The map shows some areas under construction, and some lifts and escalators by the East gate out of use.

Does the Shinkansen go to Sapporo?

Not yet. JR Hokkaido's Hokkaido Shinkansen stations are Shin-Hakodate-Hokuto, Kikonai and Okutsugaru-Imabetsu, and JR describes its work at Sapporo as towards the opening of the Sapporo station. From Sapporo, the Limited Express Hokuto goes to Hakodate and connects with the Shinkansen.

How do I get from Sapporo Station to New Chitose Airport?

On JR Hokkaido's Airport Train. JR gives the fastest time between the airport and Sapporo as 33 minutes (Special Rapid), and IC cards such as Suica work. Check the departure boards for your platform: we did not find a current official platform list.

Where are the coin lockers at Sapporo Station?

JR's locker list (July 2026) gives about 800 lockers at Sapporo, a number that changes with the construction, and 5 lockers for skis and snowboards. We did not find a locker map, so follow the signs or ask station staff. Lockers can be used for up to 3 days, counting the first day.

Can I change between the Namboku and Toho lines at Sapporo without leaving the gates?

No. The city says you have to go out through the gates and come back in within 30 minutes. With a paper ticket, use the yellow transfer gates at the Namboku and Toho south gates.

Where is the tourist information at Sapporo Station?

The Hokkaido-Sapporo Tourist Information Center is in the JR Sapporo West Concourse, open 8:30 to 20:00 all year, in Japanese, English, Chinese and Korean. JR's own Information Desk for visitors is at the West Exit.

Can I do a day trip from Sapporo by train?

Yes. On JR Hokkaido's timetable for 24 November 2026, Otaru is 34 to 46 minutes by the Rapid Airport, Noboribetsu 70 to 82 minutes and Asahikawa 85 minutes by limited express. All seats on limited expresses are reserved, so book a seat, and JR says IC cards cannot be used to Noboribetsu, Biei or Furano. For Niseko, the booked ski bus from the North Exit takes about 3 hours.

Is the subway station called Sapporo too?

Yes, but the subway writes it in hiragana, さっぽろ, while JR writes 札幌. Both are read Sapporo.
