Osaka stations on one page

  • Two main stations: Shin-Osaka is the Shinkansen (bullet train) station. Osaka Station and Umeda, to the south, are the downtown hub for local trains and subways.
  • Between them: about 4 to 7 minutes on the JR Kyoto Line, or 3 stops on the Midosuji subway line (Shin-Osaka M13 to Umeda M16). Trains run every 5 to 10 minutes.
  • Shinkansen platforms: 20 to 27 at Shin-Osaka. Platforms 20 to 22 are for trains heading west (Okayama, Hakata); 23 to 27 are for trains heading east (Kyoto, Nagoya, Tokyo).
  • Ticket gates: the Shinkansen and the local lines have separate gates, with a transfer gate between them.
  • Tourist information: Shin-Osaka, 3F concourse (8 am to 10 pm). Osaka, 1F in front of the Central Gate (7 am to 10 pm).
  • Luggage: coin lockers at both stations, and Crosta storage and delivery counters (9 am to 8 pm).
  • Umeda is several stations: JR Osaka, Hankyu, Hanshin and three Osaka Metro stations stand side by side. Allow extra time to find the right gate.
  • Need help? Ask station staff or a tourist information counter. In an emergency, call 119.

Which station do you need?

North Osaka has several big stations with similar names. JNTO says they confuse locals and visitors alike. The short version: Shin-Osaka is where the Shinkansen (bullet trains) stop. Osaka Station and Umeda, to the south, are the downtown hub for local trains, subways and private railways. If you arrive by bullet train, you get off at Shin-Osaka and then take a local train or the subway into the city.

Shin-Osaka: the bullet train station

Shin-Osaka is where Osaka's Shinkansen (bullet trains) stop. It also has JR local lines and a station on the Midosuji subway line (M13). The Osaka tourism board puts the Shinkansen and JR lines on the 3rd floor and the subway on the 2nd floor.

Which platform?

PlatformsTrains headingStops include
20 to 22WestOkayama, Hakata

Your ticket and the departure boards show your platform, and platforms can change, so check before you walk up.

  • Two sets of ticket gates. JR West's map shows a Shinkansen concourse and a separate local-line concourse, with their own ticket gates and a transfer ticket gate between them. If you change from a local train to the Shinkansen, use the transfer gate.
  • Lift to platform 20. JR West says you can go from the Shinkansen concourse to the platform for track 20 by lift.
  • Tourist information and rail passes. The Travel Service Center SHIN-OSAKA is on the 3rd floor concourse, outside the central gate for the Shinkansen lines (8 am to 10 pm daily). JR West lists tourist and railway information and the exchange and sale of rail passes for overseas visitors.
  • Japan Rail Pass. According to JR West's FAQ, the Japan Rail Pass is not valid on Nozomi and Mizuho trains. Check the train name on your ticket.
  • Collecting a reserved ticket. JR West says reserved tickets can be collected at machines and ticket offices that show the 5489 Service sign.
  • Buses to Osaka (Itami) Airport. JR West's map marks the bus stop. Itami is for domestic flights only.

Osaka and Umeda: the station maze

Osaka Station and Umeda are one huge area with many stations side by side. JNTO says the complex, including six neighbouring Hankyu, Hanshin and subway stations, handled about 2.3 million people a day (2017 figure). The names differ by company: JR's station is called Osaka, Hankyu and Hanshin call theirs Osaka-Umeda, and the Midosuji subway station is Umeda.

The 30-minute rule: Osaka Metro treats Umeda, Higashi-Umeda and Nishi-Umeda as three separate stations. If you leave the ticket gates to walk from one to another, Osaka Metro says the transfer must be made within 30 minutes.

From the Umeda subway station (M16) to the other stations

  • Exits 8 and 9: JR Osaka Station, East Gate.
  • Exit 6: Hankyu line and Hankyu Department Store.
  • Exit 7: Nishi-Umeda (Yotsubashi Line) and the Hanshin line.
  • Exits 17 and 18: Hanshin Umeda and Hanshin Department Store.

Inside JR Osaka Station

JR West's station map names the Central Gate, the Midosuji Gate (north and south) and the Sakurabashi Gate, plus the Umekita Underground Gate (B1F) with underground platforms (B2F) and platforms on the 2nd floor. It also marks coin lockers, a baby care room, a baggage room, a JR Expressway Bus ticket centre, free public wireless LAN and wheelchair-accessible toilets. Eki Marche Osaka, LUCUA, Daimaru Umeda and Grand Front Osaka are shown around the station.

Tourist information

  • Tourist Information OSAKA: 1st floor, in front of the JR Central Gate, 7 am to 10 pm daily, with English, Chinese and Korean. The Travel Service Center OSAKA next door has a travel agency and baggage storage.
  • Hankyu Tourist Center Osaka-Umeda: 1st floor of Hankyu's station, with English, Chinese, Korean and Japanese, and free Wi-Fi.

Osaka Amazing Pass: sales of this pass ended on 31 March 2024, so do not plan around it.

Sources: JNTO: Osaka Station, Osaka Metro: transfers between the three Umeda stations, Umeda station exits, JR West: Osaka Station map and Tourist Information OSAKA. Checked 19 November 2026.

A train card (ICOCA)

A tap-on travel card saves you buying a ticket for every train, subway or bus ride. In Kansai the local card is ICOCA, from JR West.

  • Where to buy: Osaka Metro says it sells ICOCA at its pink ticket machines at all stations, with a 500 yen refundable deposit if you do not already have a card. JR West ticket machines sell ICOCA too (JR West says so for Kansai Airport). We did not find an official page listing the sales points at Shin-Osaka and Osaka, so look for the JR West machines or ask a ticket office.
  • Which trains: ICOCA works on JR trains in Kansai and on the Osaka Metro. Look for the card symbol on the ticket gate.
  • Lockers: some coin lockers at Osaka Station take ICOCA. JR West has a page to search which are free.
  • Shinkansen: a Shinkansen ticket is separate. Buy it, or collect your reserved ticket, before you go to the gate.

Arriving by air? Klook lists an ICOCA card with pick-up at Kansai Airport, a partner link. You collect it at the airport, not at Shin-Osaka or Osaka, so it only helps if you fly in. Check the listing for the pick-up counter and hours.

This changes often. Sales points, deposits and card types have changed several times in recent years. Check JR West's or Osaka Metro's page, or ask at a ticket office, before you buy.

Lockers and luggage delivery

If you do not want to carry heavy bags through a crowded station, you have three choices: a coin locker, a storage counter, or sending the bag ahead to your hotel.

  • Coin lockers. JR West says all Shinkansen stations and major stations have coin lockers. Its map marks them at Shin-Osaka, including by the Shinkansen concourse, and at Osaka Station. The Osaka tourism board lists lockers at 11 locations inside JR Osaka Station. Lockers fill up on busy days. JR West has pages to search which ICOCA lockers are free at Shin-Osaka and at Osaka Station.
  • Crosta (storage and delivery). Crosta Shin-Osaka (1st floor) and Crosta Osaka are both open 9 am to 8 pm. The Osaka tourism board lists storage at 1,000 yen per bag and same-day hotel delivery at 2,000 yen per suitcase. JR West says Crosta's luggage delivery runs between Kyoto, Shin-Osaka, Osaka, Hiroshima and Hakata stations. Its Same-day Delivery WEST sends bags on the Shinkansen, so you can drop them at one station and collect them at another later that day.
  • TiS Osaka Branch. In front of the Central Gate ticket gate at Osaka Station (10 am to 7 pm), listed at 1,000 yen per suitcase by the Osaka tourism board. The Travel Service Center beside Tourist Information OSAKA also lists baggage storage.
  • Have these ready: your hotel name, address and phone number, written down.
  • Keep with you: medicines, valuables, chargers and a change of clothes.

Prefer to book it online? Klook lists an Osaka and Kyoto luggage delivery service, a partner link. Check the listing for pick-up points, sizes, prices and cut-off times.

Sources: Osaka tourism board pages for Shin-Osaka and Osaka/Umeda, and JR West: Hands-Free Travel. Checked 19 November 2026. The tourism board pages are some months old, so hours, prices and counters may have changed: confirm at the counter.

Help for older travellers and anyone who needs assistance

You do not have to cope alone. Asking early is easier than struggling.

  • Tourist information. At Shin-Osaka, the Travel Service Center is on the 3rd floor concourse (8 am to 10 pm). At Osaka, Tourist Information OSAKA is on the 1st floor in front of the Central Gate (7 am to 10 pm), with staff who speak English, Chinese and Korean.
  • Lifts. JR West's station maps mark lifts. At Shin-Osaka, you can go from the Shinkansen concourse to platform 20 by lift. Osaka Metro's Umeda station page lists barrier-free routes with lifts between the platforms and the street.
  • Osaka Station. JR West's map marks a barrier-free route and wheelchair-accessible toilets.
  • Station staff. Tell staff at a ticket gate or ticket office if you need help or are not sure which way to go.
  • Send your suitcase ahead. See the luggage section above: you can have it delivered to your hotel.
  • Visitor hotline. The Japan Visitor Hotline, 050-3816-2787, is open 24 hours and helps in English, Chinese and Korean. In an emergency, call 119.

Sources: JR West: Shin-Osaka Station map, Osaka Station map and Osaka Metro: Umeda station, checked 19 November 2026.

Leaving Osaka by train

  • Know your station. Shinkansen leave from Shin-Osaka. Local trains and subways run from both Shin-Osaka and Osaka/Umeda.
  • To Kansai Airport. The Haruka limited express runs from Shin-Osaka in about 50 minutes. From Osaka Station, the Kansai Airport Rapid takes about 65 minutes, and the airport limousine bus from Osaka Station (Umeda) takes about 60 minutes and costs 1,800 yen one way (bus company's fare). JR West's discounted WEST QR HARUKA ticket for visitors with Temporary Visitor status was listed at 1,900 yen to Osaka or Shin-Osaka (adult) when we checked on 18 November 2026. See our Kansai Airport guide.
  • Haruka tickets online. Klook lists a JR Haruka ticket (partner link) with Osaka and Shin-Osaka departures. It is for visitors with Temporary Visitor status, not for Japanese passport holders or permanent residents.
  • To Namba. Take the Midosuji subway line from Shin-Osaka or Umeda. It is about 20 minutes from Shin-Osaka with no change (JNTO).
  • To Kyoto. The JR Kyoto Line serves Osaka Station, and the Shinkansen to Kyoto leave from Shin-Osaka.
  • Allow time for the stations themselves. Umeda and Shin-Osaka are big. We did not find official walking times between gates, so we give none: add a buffer, especially with luggage.
  • Last trains. We did not confirm last-train times, so check the timetable for your route before a late evening journey.
  • Taking a Shinkansen? Check your platform on the departure board, and your train name against your pass. The Japan Rail Pass is not valid on Nozomi and Mizuho.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which station do I use in Osaka: Shin-Osaka, Osaka or Umeda?

Shin-Osaka is where the Shinkansen (bullet trains) stop. Osaka Station and Umeda, to the south, are the downtown hub for local trains, subways and private railways. The Japan National Tourism Organization (JNTO) says the similar names confuse locals and visitors alike. If you arrive by Shinkansen, you get off at Shin-Osaka and then take a local train or the subway into the city.

How do I get from Shin-Osaka to Umeda or Osaka Station?

Two easy ways. The JR Kyoto Line takes about 4 to 7 minutes to Osaka Station (our sources differ). The Midosuji subway line goes from Shin-Osaka (M13) to Umeda (M16) in 3 stops and carries on to Namba. The Osaka tourism board says JR and Osaka Metro trains run every 5 to 10 minutes between Shin-Osaka and Osaka.

Which platforms are the Shinkansen at Shin-Osaka?

Shinkansen platforms are numbered 20 to 27. JR Central's train information lists platforms 20 to 22 for trains heading west (toward Okayama and Hakata) and 23 to 27 for trains heading east (toward Kyoto, Nagoya and Tokyo). Your ticket and the departure boards show your platform, and platforms can change.

Are the Shinkansen and local-line ticket gates the same?

No. Shin-Osaka has a Shinkansen concourse and a separate local-line concourse, with their own ticket gates and a transfer ticket gate between them, according to JR West. A lift runs from the Shinkansen concourse to platform 20.

Is Umeda one station or several?

Several. JR's station is called Osaka. Hankyu and Hanshin call theirs Osaka-Umeda. Osaka Metro has three separate stations: Umeda (M16, Midosuji Line), Higashi-Umeda (T20, Tanimachi Line) and Nishi-Umeda (Y11, Yotsubashi Line). JNTO says the complex, with six neighbouring Hankyu, Hanshin and subway stations, handled about 2.3 million people a day in 2017.

Which Umeda subway exit leads to JR Osaka Station?

From Osaka Metro's Umeda station (M16), exit 3A leads to the JR Osaka Station Midosuji North Gate, and exits 8 and 9 lead to the JR East Gate. Exit 6 leads to Hankyu, exit 7 to Nishi-Umeda and Hanshin, and exits 17 and 18 to Hanshin. If you leave the ticket gates to walk between Umeda, Higashi-Umeda and Nishi-Umeda, Osaka Metro says the transfer must be made within 30 minutes.

Where is the tourist information at the stations?

At Shin-Osaka, the Travel Service Center SHIN-OSAKA is on the 3rd floor concourse, outside the central gate for the Shinkansen lines (8 am to 10 pm daily). At Osaka, Tourist Information OSAKA is on the 1st floor in front of the JR Central Gate (7 am to 10 pm daily), with staff who speak English, Chinese and Korean. Hankyu Tourist Center Osaka-Umeda is on the 1st floor of Hankyu's station.

Where can I leave my luggage?

Both stations have coin lockers, and JR West says all Shinkansen stations and major stations do. Crosta has counters at Shin-Osaka and Osaka (9 am to 8 pm). The Osaka tourism board lists storage at 1,000 yen per bag and same-day hotel delivery at 2,000 yen per suitcase, but that page is some months old, so confirm at the counter.

Do I need an ICOCA card?

You do not need one, but it saves buying a ticket for every ride. ICOCA is the local card from JR West, and Osaka Metro says it sells ICOCA at its pink ticket machines, with a 500 yen refundable deposit. Card types and sales points change, so check before you buy.

Does the Japan Rail Pass work on every Shinkansen from Shin-Osaka?

No. According to JR West's FAQ, the Japan Rail Pass is not valid on Nozomi and Mizuho trains. Check your pass and the train name before you board.

How do I get from Osaka to Kansai Airport?

From Shin-Osaka, the Haruka limited express takes about 50 minutes. From Osaka Station, the Kansai Airport Rapid takes about 65 minutes, and the airport limousine bus from Osaka Station (Umeda) takes about 60 minutes (1,800 yen one way, according to the bus company). Our Kansai Airport guide has the details. Fares and times change, so check the operator.

Where do I collect a ticket I booked online?

JR West says reserved tickets can be collected at ticket machines and Midori-no-madoguchi ticket offices that show the 5489 Service sign. For rail pass exchange and receipt, JR West lists Shin-Osaka Station and Osaka Station (6 am to 10 pm) and Travel Service Center SHIN-OSAKA (8 am to 10 pm).
