New Chitose Airport (CTS) is the airport for Sapporo and the start of most ski trips and Snow Festival visits in Hokkaido. It has two terminal buildings, a JR station underneath and buses at the door, and in winter the weather can change your plans. Until about the end of June 2027 the 3F passage between the terminals is closed, so the way across is the 2F corridor. This guide is written in plain English so that a first-time visitor, a family or an older traveller can follow it step by step. You can print the one-page card below and carry it with you.

New Chitose Airport on one page

  • Two terminals: International flights use the International Terminal (west), domestic flights the Domestic Terminal (east).
  • Between them: the 2F Connecting Corridor. Part of the 3F passage is closed until about the end of June 2027.
  • Train to Sapporo: JR New Chitose Airport Station, B1F under the Domestic Terminal. Fastest 33 minutes (JR). Car 4 is the reserved “U seat” car.
  • Bus to Sapporo: Domestic 1F stops 14 and 22, International stop 84. About 70 minutes to Sapporo Station. Buses leave on time even if your flight is late.
  • Money: International 2F exchange counter (8:30 to 19:00) and a Seven Bank ATM that takes overseas cards (6:00 to 21:00).
  • Buildings open: Domestic 5:00 to 23:30, International 5:00 to 21:00. No staying overnight except in the airport hotels.
  • Snow or a cancelled flight? Contact your airline, and check Hokkaido travel safety information.
  • Help: information centers in both terminals. Airport line 0123-23-0111 (7:00 to 23:00). Emergency: 119.

Which terminal? Domestic and International, floor by floor

New Chitose has two terminal buildings joined by a connecting facility: the Domestic Terminal on the east side and the International Terminal on the west side. Your ticket and the departure boards tell you which one you need. This is how the airport's floor guide (information as of 17 September 2026) lays them out.

  • Domestic arrivals by airline. The floor guide shows Arrival Gates 1 to 3 for ANA, AIR DO, IBEX and Peach, Gate 4 for JAL, and Gate 5 for JAL, SKYMARK, FDA, Jetstar and Spring Japan.
  • Domestic 1F notices. The airport says part of the Arrivals Lobby cannot be reached, so use the outside walkway, or go up to 2F by escalator or lift. There is no way between the Main and Center buildings on 1F, because that area is a road for vehicles; go via the 2F Departure Lobby.
  • Taxis. The Sapporo Tourist Association lists two taxi stands outside the Domestic Terminal exits and one outside the International Terminal exit.

Floors and counters can change, so follow the signs.

Between the terminals: the 2F corridor (3F closed)

⚠️ The 3F passage between the terminals is closed until about the end of June 2027

The airport's notice (posted 9 November 2026): because of construction, part of the 3rd-floor passage in the connecting facility is closed, and you cannot go between the Domestic and International Terminals on 3F. The closure runs from 2 September 2026 to the end of June 2027 (the airport says this end date is tentative).

Use the 2F Connecting Corridor. It has moving walkways, signed “To Domestic Terminal Building” and “To International Terminal Building”.

  • Landed on an international flight and taking the train? Arrivals are on International 2F. Walk the 2F corridor to the Domestic Terminal, carry on past the souvenir shops to the lift, and go down to B1F for JR. That is JR's own route (its February 2024 leaflet).
  • Flying out internationally from the JR station? Go up to the Domestic Terminal 2F, walk the corridor to the International Terminal, then up to 3F for departures.
  • How far? The airport does not state a walking time. JR's leaflet gives the walkway as 240 metres in its text and 300 metres on its map, so think of it as a few hundred metres and allow extra time with luggage or children.
  • Corridor hours. 5:00 to 23:30.
  • No free Wi-Fi in the corridor. The airport's free Wi-Fi covers both terminals, but not the connecting facilities.

Construction dates can change, so follow the signs.

To Sapporo by train: the Airport Train

JR Hokkaido runs the “Airport Train” (the Rapid Airport) straight from the airport to Sapporo, and some trains go on to Otaru. JR New Chitose Airport Station (AP15) is on B1F of the Domestic Terminal; the ticket gates and the JR Information Desk are on B1F, and the platforms on B2F.

  • How often and how fast. JR: “six Airport Trains per hour between 9:00 and 16:00 from New Chitose Airport to Sapporo”: one Special Rapid (fastest 33 minutes), three Rapid trains (fastest 37 minutes) and two Semi-Rapid trains (fastest 43 minutes). Fastest to Otaru: 73 minutes.
  • Stops. Minami-Chitose (H14) and Shin-Sapporo (H05) for every type; Chitose (H13) for the Rapid and Semi-Rapid; then Sapporo (01). Teine, Minami-Otaru and Otaru for the trains that go on.
  • Seats. Cars 1 to 3 and 5 to 6 are non-reserved: no booking needed, and you can board any Airport Train that day. Car 4 is the reserved “U seat” car (43 seats): you need a reserved-seat ticket as well as the fare, and you cannot enter car 4 without one.
  • IC cards work. JR lists Suica, PASMO, TOICA, manaca, ICOCA, PiTaPa, SUGOCA, nimoca, Hayakaken and Kitaca. Not for Yoichi, Kutchan or Niseko: buy a ticket at the station first.
  • Paper tickets. The ticket machines take cash only; the reserved-seat machine and the ticket counter take cash and credit cards. No QR code payments. A ticket is for one ride: leave the gates on the way and you need a new one.
  • Passport-reader ticket machines. JR Hokkaido has posted a notice (8 October 2026) with important notes on its ticket machines with passport readers at Sapporo and New Chitose Airport stations. We did not read it, so check it on JR’s English home page before you rely on those machines.
  • IC cards elsewhere. JR’s English home page also notes that IC cards can be used only at some stations, so check before travelling beyond the Airport Train route.
  • Wi-Fi on board. JR Hokkaido says it provides Wi-Fi on the Airport Train cars and at stations.
  • Booked a reserved seat online? JR says reserved-seat e-tickets do not open the gates: go through with an IC card or a paper fare ticket.
  • Luggage. Within 250 cm in total (2 m at most in length), up to 2 items, each up to 30 kg.
  • Ticket office. 6:30 to 22:00. Reserved-seat machines from 6:30 until the last train. Tour counter 9:00 to 17:30.
  • First and last trains. JR Hokkaido’s timetable for 24 November 2026 (a weekday): from New Chitose Airport, the first train to Sapporo is the Rapid Airport at 6:38 (Sapporo 7:18), and the last is the Special Rapid Airport at 23:21 (Sapporo 23:59). From Sapporo to the airport, the first is the Rapid Airport at 5:50 (airport 6:28), and the last is a local train at 22:25, which stops everywhere and reaches the airport at 23:12. Timetables change, so check JR’s departures the day before.
  • Rail passes for visitors. JR Hokkaido’s Sapporo–Noboribetsu Area Pass and Sapporo–Furano Area Pass (4 consecutive days each) include New Chitose Airport, and the Hokkaido Rail Pass (5, 7 or 10 days) covers all JR Hokkaido lines except the Hokkaido Shinkansen. JR says all non-Japanese passport holders can use them; show your passport itself when you buy or exchange one (a copy is not accepted). With a pass you can sit in the reserved car or the non-reserved cars. Put only the pass into the ticket gate and take it back: JR does not reissue a lost pass. The passes do not cover the subway.
  • Going on to Hakodate, Noboribetsu, Tomamu or Kushiro? Change at Minami-Chitose, where the limited express trains leave from the same platform. JR says all seats on those limited expresses are reserved.
  • Going beyond Sapporo? JR says IC cards cannot be used to Noboribetsu, Biei, Furano or Toya, and some cards, such as SAPICA, do not work on its trains. On one-man trains (one or two cars, no conductor) press the button to open the door; with no ticket, take a numbered ticket and pay the driver in cash. Skis and snowboards must be fully in their bags, and JR warns that paper tickets kept in a phone case can stop working. Lost something? JR’s “find chat” searches for items lost on its trains, in English and Chinese.
  • Need help with a wheelchair? Many JR stations have no staff or no lift, so JR recommends asking for help in advance with its inquiry form. Platforms are slippery with snow in winter.
  • Going on to Otaru from Sapporo? JR says trains for “Teine” or “Hoshimi” do not continue to Otaru.
If the trains stop. JR asks you to check the boards and announcements (some are only in Japanese, so use a translation app) and to consider other transport: the airport bus (about 70 to 90 minutes) or a taxi (about 60 minutes). It warns that buses can get extremely crowded when trains are cancelled, and that times grow or services stop in bad weather. With a paper ticket, a cancelled trip is refunded in full, and the station can give you a delay certificate. JR says it does not pay for meals, other transport or hotels when trains are late or stopped.

Arriving in Sapporo? Our Sapporo Station guide covers the gates during the Shinkansen works, the subway to Odori and Susukino, and the lockers.

Times change, so check JR Hokkaido.

To Sapporo by bus

  • Where to buy. Domestic 1F: the Hokkaido Chuo Bus counter near Arrival Gate 4, and the Hokuto Kotsu and Donan Bus counter near Arrival Gate 2. International: ticket machines in the 1F Main Entrance Lobby (not for every route).
  • The bus leaves on time. Hokkaido Chuo Bus says its buses leave on schedule even if flights are delayed, and that weather and traffic can make them late, so allow time before a flight.
  • Big bags go in the trunk: tell the staff your stop. If the bus is full, you may be offered a fold-down seat or the next bus.

Timetables change with the season, so check the bus company.

Ski buses: Niseko, Rusutsu and Furano

Going straight to the snow? These leave from the Domestic Terminal.

  • Niseko ski bus (Hokkaido Chuo Bus with Niseko Bus), booking required. It runs from 12 December 2026 to 22 March 2027; the bus companies say the first and last days depend on the snow. It boards at the Domestic Terminal only: the airport lists stop 12.
  • Times (2026-27 timetable). From the airport at 10:00, 13:40, 14:40 and 15:30, at Niseko Hirafu (Welcome Center) at 13:33, 17:13, 18:13 and 19:03, after stops at Niseko View Plaza, Hon-dori, Annupuri, Niseko Village and Higashiyama. Back from Hirafu at 8:00, 9:00, 10:00 and 15:30, at the airport at 11:28, 12:28, 13:28 and 18:58. About 3 hours 30 minutes, with a rest stop.
  • How to book. Online in English, or by phone at the Chuo Bus Sapporo Terminal, 0570-200-600 (7:30 to 18:00), by 18:00 the day before. Seats are not numbered. Online bookings are not paid online: buy the ticket at a counter on the day, or pay cash on the bus.
  • Late or delayed flight? The last bus to Niseko leaves at 15:30, and the bus company says buses can be late in bad weather. Have a back-up: the Airport Train to Sapporo, then the morning ski bus from Sapporo Station (7:50 and 9:20; see our Sapporo Station guide).
  • Ski gear. No ski or snowboard boots on board. Put skis and snowboards in a case (sole guards are fine for snowboards).
  • Rusutsu. The airport lists Rusutsu with the ski bus at stop 12. The bus company lists a fare from New Chitose Airport to Rusutsu Resort Hotel but no times, so ask it before you rely on it.
  • Furano (HOKKAIDO BUS): domestic stop 23. We could not open the bus company’s site, so we give no times. In winter, JR also reaches Furano by train via Asahikawa.
  • Noboribetsu Onsen (hot springs, not skiing): Donan Bus runs a direct bus, booking required (up to 2 hours before), about 1 hour 15 minutes. On 24 November 2026 its page listed departures at 13:20, 14:10 and 15:10 from International Terminal stop 86, then Domestic Terminal stops 29 and 2 a few minutes later. Back from Noboribetsu Onsen at 9:55, 10:30 and 11:45.
  • Landed at the International Terminal? Walk the 2F corridor to the Domestic Terminal; the domestic bus stops are on 1F.
  • Skis and snowboards count as large items at the baggage counters (Domestic 1F, 7:00 to 20:00).

Ski-bus dates and times change every season.

Money: currency exchange and ATMs

The airport says its currency exchange opens in line with the flights. These are the counters, machines and ATMs it lists.

  • International 2F (arrivals). Currency exchange counter in the arrival lobby, 8:30 to 19:00 (may change with flight times), and an automatic exchange machine. ATMs: Seven Bank, 6:00 to 21:00, all year, takes overseas cards; AEON Bank. A bank counter (North Pacific Bank), weekdays only.
  • International 3F (departures). Exchange counter in the north departure lobby, 9:30 to 16:00 (the south one is suspended). ATMs (North Pacific Bank, AEON Bank) on the floor guide.
  • Domestic 1F. Exchange counters in the arrival lobby: ANA side 7:15 to 20:00, JAL side 9:00 to 17:00. Automatic exchange machines on the north and south sides, open with the building.
  • Domestic ATMs, north and south sides. Seven Bank, 6:20 to 23:00, and Japan Post Bank (hours vary by side and day) both take overseas cards. AEON Bank and Lawson Bank also take overseas cards and follow the building hours.

Hours can change with flight times.

Hours differ by counter: for example 9:00 to 19:00 and 9:00 to 17:00 at International 2F, and 9:00 to 18:00 at Domestic 1F (closed 13:00 to 14:00). Pick-up and return machines follow the building hours.

Lockers, big bags and hotel delivery

Skiing, or carrying winter gear? The airport has lockers on almost every floor, a place for bags too big for a locker, and counters that send bags on to Sapporo.

  • Coin lockers. Large, medium and small, on Domestic B1F to 4F, International 1F to 4F and in the connecting corridor (1F to 3F). Up to 3 days; for longer, use the baggage storage counters. The airport says lockers are charged by the 12 hours. The exact spots are on the airport's online map, so follow the signs.
  • Too big for a locker? The Baggage Port takes large carry-on cases and strollers, unlocked with your IC card, for up to 3 days. It is cashless: transport IC cards and QR code payments.
  • JR lockers. JR also has coin lockers in the JR station on B1F (JR's locker list, July 2026).
  • Baggage counters, Domestic Terminal. 1F, 7:00 to 20:00: storage, home delivery and porters. Golf bags, skis and snowboards count as large. 2F, 7:30 to 20:00.
  • Baggage counters, International Terminal. 2F, 9:00 to 18:00: storage and same-day delivery. Baggage wrapping on 3F, 11:00 to 14:00.
  • Same-day delivery to your Sapporo hotel. To designated hotels in Sapporo's Chuo Ward, from Domestic 1F: near Arrival Gate 4 (open 8:00 to 20:00; same-day hotel delivery accepted 8:00 to 14:30) and near Arrival Gate 2 (8:00 to 14:30). Ask whether your hotel is on the list.
  • On the bus. Big bags go in the trunk. Hokkaido Chuo Bus takes bags under 30 kg, under 0.25 m³ and under 2 m long.
  • On the train. JR's limit on the Airport Train: within 250 cm in total (2 m at most in length), up to 2 items, each up to 30 kg. Some items may not be allowed.

Hours can change with flight times.

Opening hours, hotels, onsen and the observation deck

  • Building hours. Domestic Terminal 5:00 to 23:30. International Terminal 5:00 to 21:00. Connecting corridor 5:00 to 23:30. The airport says hours can change with flight delays, and on days with late-night domestic flights only some areas stay open later.
  • No staying overnight. After closing you may not rest in the airport, except in the accommodation facilities.
  • Airport hotels. Air Terminal Hotel, in the Domestic Terminal by Arrival Gate 5 (its own lift, 1F to 5F). Portom International Hokkaido, on International 4F: enter by the escalator on the 3F departure lobby side, or by the hotel lift from 1F to 3F.
  • In Chitose city. JR Hokkaido lists JR Inn Chitose among its group hotels; the Rapid and Semi-Rapid Airport Trains stop at Chitose (H13). Rooms and prices (partner link)
  • New Chitose Airport Onsen. Domestic 4F. The airport describes it as a relaxation facility using natural hot spring water, where you can stay a long time. Open 23 hours, 10:00 to 9:00 the next morning (last entry 7:30). A late-night surcharge applies after 1:00. Between 23:00 and 5:00, when the terminal is closed, use the 24-hour lift from 1F to 4F.
  • Observation deck. Domestic 4F, entered from the 3F food area, beside the Airport History Museum. Open 1 April to 30 November, 8:00 to 20:00; closed December to March. It may also close in strong wind or heavy snow.

Hours can change, so check the airport's pages.

Snow, delays and cancelled flights

Hokkaido winters are cold and snowy. The Sapporo Tourist Association recommends warm clothing and shoes with good grip on snowy roads. This is what the official pages say to do when the weather gets in the way.

  • Your flight. The airport's FAQ: for seat availability, bookings, cancellations and missed flights, contact the airline you are flying with.
  • Delays and transport. The airport points you to the airlines' and transport companies' own websites, and to the Hokkaido District Transport Bureau's Hokkaido travel safety information.
  • Live train information. Check JR Hokkaido’s Train Operation Information (English), or its information for each station, which also shows that day’s departures. JR’s guide to it describes delays of 5 minutes or more by station (5:00 to 25:00), a map of cancellations and delays of 30 minutes or more by line, including the Chitose Line (Sapporo, New Chitose Airport, Tomakomai), 5:00 to 24:00, and train locations with delays of 1 minute or more (5:00 to 25:00).
  • Trains stopped? JR Hokkaido lists the airport bus (about 70 to 90 minutes) and taxis (about 60 minutes) as alternatives, and warns they can get extremely crowded.
  • Buses in snow. Hokkaido Chuo Bus says weather and traffic can delay the bus, and that it leaves on time even when flights are late.
  • Stuck late at night? The terminals close (Domestic 23:30, International 21:00) and you may not stay inside after closing, except in the accommodation facilities. There is a hotel in each terminal, and the New Chitose Airport Onsen (Domestic 4F) is open 23 hours, 10:00 to 9:00 the next morning.
  • Observation deck is closed December to March.

We did not find airport pages about snow clearing or what the airport provides on a snowbound night, so we give no advice on that. Ask your airline and the information centers.

Rules can change, so check your airline.

Information and help

You do not have to cope alone. Asking early is easier than struggling, especially in snow.

  • Information centers. Domestic Terminal: 1F in front of Arrival Gates 2, 4 and 5; 2F departure lobby (north and south) and Center Plaza. International Terminal: 1F curbside lobby, 2F arrival lobby, 3F departure lobby (north and south) and inside the 3F boarding area. The airport says they open in line with the flights. They give flight, transport and Hokkaido tourist information and lend wheelchairs free of charge.
  • Hokkaido Foreign Tourist Information Center. International Terminal 2F, 8:00 to 20:00, all year. Help in English, Chinese and Korean (Thai, Vietnamese, Portuguese and Spanish through a three-way interpreter service): travel advice, sightseeing and transport, and pointers to places to stay, duty-free shops, car rental and Wi-Fi.
  • Hotel bookings in Hokkaido. At the information centers at Domestic 1F, Arrival Gates 2 and 5, and International 2F, arrival lobby.
  • JR Information Desk. On B1F by the JR ticket gates. JR lists help in English, Chinese and Korean, rail passes and tickets, 8:30 to 19:00 (hours from a February 2024 JR leaflet, so check).
  • Airport phone line. 0123-23-0111, 7:00 to 23:00, all year, in Japanese and English (it starts with an automated voice menu).
  • Prayer room. International Terminal 2F, on the airport's floor guide. We did not find one in the Domestic Terminal.
  • Moving walkways. The airport says the connecting corridor has moving walkways for people who find walking difficult.
  • Emergency. Call 119 for an ambulance or fire, 110 for the police.

Counters and hours can change.

Leaving from New Chitose: a checklist

  • Which terminal? International departures are on International 3F; domestic departures on Domestic 2F.
  • Coming by train? You arrive at B1F under the Domestic Terminal. For an international flight, go up to 2F and walk the 2F corridor (the 3F passage is closed until about the end of June 2027), then up to 3F.
  • Early flight? The first Airport Train from Sapporo leaves at 5:50 and reaches the airport at 6:28 (JR’s timetable for 24 November 2026). The first bus from Sapporo was not on the timetable we read. Check JR and the bus companies the day before. The terminals open at 5:00.
  • Snow forecast? Contact your airline, check Hokkaido travel safety information, and leave earlier: trains and buses can be delayed and crowded.
  • Collect your bags. Lockers hold bags for up to 3 days; check the counter hours if you left bags at a baggage counter.
  • Leftover yen? The International 3F exchange counter (north) is open 9:30 to 16:00.
  • Flying on from Tokyo? See our Haneda Airport guide or Narita Airport guide.

Times change, so check the operator.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get from New Chitose Airport to Sapporo?

By JR train or by bus. JR Hokkaido's Airport Train leaves from JR New Chitose Airport Station, on B1F under the Domestic Terminal. JR gives six trains an hour between 9:00 and 16:00, and 33 minutes for the fastest (the Special Rapid). The airport buses to Sapporo city centre take about 70 minutes to Sapporo Station on the bus company's timetable.

How long is the train from New Chitose Airport to Sapporo?

JR Hokkaido gives the fastest times as 33 minutes on the Special Rapid, 37 minutes on the Rapid and 43 minutes on the Semi-Rapid.

Which terminal am I in at New Chitose Airport?

International flights use the International Terminal Building, on the west side. Domestic flights use the Domestic Terminal Building, on the east side. A connecting corridor joins them. The JR station is under the Domestic Terminal, on B1F.

How do I walk between the international and domestic terminals?

Use the 2F Connecting Corridor, which has moving walkways. Part of the 3F passage in the connecting facility is closed for construction from 2 September 2026 until about the end of June 2027, and during that time you cannot go between the terminals on 3F. The airport does not state a walking time, so allow extra time with luggage.

Where do the buses to Sapporo leave from?

The airport lists the Sapporo city centre buses at stops 14 and 22 outside the Domestic Terminal (1F), and stop 84 at the International Terminal. The bus company says the buses leave on time even if flights are delayed.

Can I use Suica or another IC card at New Chitose Airport?

On the JR Airport Train to Sapporo, yes: JR Hokkaido lists Suica, PASMO, Kitaca and other IC cards. Not beyond Otaru, for Yoichi, Kutchan or Niseko. On the airport buses, Hokkaido Chuo Bus says transport IC cards work on its own buses only, not on Hokuto Kotsu buses.

Is there a bus from New Chitose Airport to Niseko or Furano?

Yes. The Niseko ski bus (booking required) runs from 12 December 2026 to 22 March 2027 from domestic stop 12: on its 2026-27 timetable it leaves at 10:00, 13:40, 14:40 and 15:30 and takes about 3 hours 30 minutes to Niseko Hirafu. Book by 18:00 the day before. The airport lists a bus to Furano from domestic stop 23; we could not read its timetable, so check the bus company's own page.

Can I stay overnight at New Chitose Airport?

The airport says you may not stay in the terminal after it closes, except in the accommodation facilities. The Domestic Terminal is open 5:00 to 23:30 and the International Terminal 5:00 to 21:00. The New Chitose Airport Onsen on Domestic 4F is open 23 hours, from 10:00 to 9:00 the next morning.

What happens at New Chitose Airport when it snows?

For your flight, the airport says to contact your airline. For delays and transport disruption, it points to the airlines, the transport operators and the Hokkaido District Transport Bureau's Hokkaido travel safety information. If trains stop, JR Hokkaido lists the airport bus and taxis as alternatives, but says they can get extremely crowded.

Where can I change money or use an ATM at New Chitose Airport?

International 2F has a currency exchange counter (8:30 to 19:00) and a Seven Bank ATM (6:00 to 21:00) that takes overseas cards. The Domestic Terminal has exchange counters on 1F and ATMs on its north and south sides, including Seven Bank and Japan Post Bank, which take overseas cards.

Can I send my luggage to my hotel in Sapporo?

Yes, to designated hotels in Sapporo's Chuo Ward. The airport lists same-day delivery from Domestic 1F (near Arrival Gate 4, accepted 8:00 to 14:30, and near Arrival Gate 2, 8:00 to 14:30). The International 2F baggage counter (9:00 to 18:00) also lists same-day delivery.
