Kansai Airport on one page

  • Where: Kansai Airport (KIX) serves Osaka, Kyoto, Nara and Kobe. By train it is about 34 to 43 minutes to Namba, about 50 minutes by Haruka to Shin-Osaka, and about 75 minutes by Haruka to Kyoto.
  • Terminals: there are 2. International flights arrive at Terminal 1. Peach, Jeju Air and Spring Airlines use Terminal 2. A free shuttle bus links them.
  • Before you fly: register on Visit Japan Web (free) and keep the QR codes on your phone. Every traveller, children included, needs their own QR code.
  • After landing: immigration, then baggage, then customs, then the arrival area on the 1st floor.
  • In the arrival area: the Kansai Tourist Information Center (9 am to 7 pm), SIM vending machines and Wi-Fi counters, currency exchange, cash machines, and the bus stops. The train station is one floor up, on the 2nd floor.
  • Free Wi-Fi: connect to AirportFreeWiFi-Kansai for quick messages.
  • Need help? Call the Kansai Tourist Information Center on +81 72-456-6160 (9 am to 7 pm). In an emergency, call 119.

Which terminal?

Kansai Airport has two terminals. Check your ticket or airline app, because airlines can change terminals.

Terminal 2Peach, Jeju Air and Spring Airlines.Airport limousine buses stop at both terminals. For the trains, use the free shuttle bus to Terminal 1.
Moving between terminals: a free shuttle bus links Terminal 1 and Terminal 2. The stop for Terminal 2 is on the 1st floor of Aeroplaza. See the airport's connections guide and Terminal 2 access page for the current details. Allow extra time for the transfer.

Flying to Osaka's other airport? Osaka International Airport (Itami, ITM) is for domestic flights only, and Kansai Airport's own site sends international travellers to KIX. We do not cover Itami.

Your first hour after landing

Most people follow the same steps. Signs are in Japanese and English, and staff are nearby if you are unsure. International flights arrive at Terminal 1, on the 1st floor.

  1. Before you fly (optional but helpful). Register on the free Visit Japan Web site. You enter your immigration and customs details once and get QR codes. Take a screenshot of them in case your signal is weak.
  2. Immigration. Have your passport ready, and your arrival card if you filled one in. Kansai has combined immigration and customs kiosks at Terminals 1 and 2 (since April 2025): scan your Visit Japan Web QR code there. Follow the signs and the staff. The automatic gates do not stamp your passport, so ask staff after the gate if you would like a stamp.
  3. Baggage claim. Find your flight on the screens and collect your bags. If a bag is missing or damaged, go to your airline staff with your baggage tag.
  4. Customs. Show your Visit Japan Web QR code, or a paper declaration form (many flights hand these out). If you carry meat, fruit, plants or animals, declare them.

Then you arrive in the arrival area on the 1st floor. Here you find the Kansai Tourist Information Center on the south side (9 am to 7 pm), SIM vending machines, Wi-Fi counters, currency exchange, cash machines and the bus stops. Kansai Airport Station is one floor up, on the 2nd floor of Terminal 1.

Rules change. Immigration and customs procedures are updated often. If anything here differs from what the signs or officers tell you, follow the signs and officers.

Sources: Kansai Airport: connections guide, Kansai Tourist Information Center and currency exchange. Checked 18 November 2026.

A train card (ICOCA)

A tap-on travel card saves you buying a ticket for every train, subway or bus ride. In Kansai the local card is ICOCA, from JR West.

  • Where at Kansai Airport: the airport says ICOCA is sold at the JR or Nankai Kansai Airport Station, outside Terminal 1 on the 2nd floor, and JR West ticket machines sell it too. Sales points and card types change, so check the airport's ICOCA page before you travel.
  • Which trains: ICOCA works on JR trains in Kansai. Look for the card symbol on the ticket gate.
  • Going on to Kyoto: Kyoto's city buses and subway accept ICOCA, Suica, PASMO and PiTaPa, according to the Kyoto City Transportation Bureau.
  • Already have a Suica or PASMO? It works on Kyoto's buses and subway too, so you do not need a second card for those.
  • Welcome Suica: we did not find an official page saying it is sold at Kansai Airport, so do not plan around it. ICOCA is the card to look for.
This changes often. Sales points, deposits and card types have changed several times in recent years. Check the airport's page or ask at the Kansai Tourist Information Center before you buy.

Sending your suitcase to your hotel

If you do not want to carry heavy bags onto a crowded train, you can post them ahead. Kansai Airport lists delivery counters run by Yamato and JAL ABC in Terminal 1, and a Kansai Airport Baggage Service counter on the 1st floor. There is no counter in Terminal 2: the airport says to use the 4th floor of Terminal 1.

  • Yamato. Terminal 1 north, 4th floor (6:30 am to 10:30 pm). The airport also lists a Yamato counter on the north side of the 1st floor, open from 16 November 2026 (6:30 am to 10:30 pm).
  • JAL ABC. Terminal 1 south, 4th floor (6:30 am to 11 pm, last reception 10:30 pm), and a counter at the north end of the 1st floor (6:30 am to 10:30 pm).
  • Kansai Airport Baggage Service. Terminal 1, 1st floor, before security (7 am to 9 pm). It lists Yamato, Sagawa and Japan Post as its carriers.
  • Size and weight. JAL ABC's Kansai page lists a limit of 160 cm (length, width and height added together) and 30 kg per bag, lower than at Tokyo's airports. Ask the counter before you hand your bag over, and ask for the price and the delivery day too.
  • Porter service. Someone can carry your bags from the arrival gate. The basic charge in Terminal 1 is 530 yen, and you reserve by 11 am the day before, through the JAL ABC counters.
  • Have these ready: your hotel name, address and phone number, written down.
  • Keep with you: medicines, valuables, chargers and a change of clothes.

Prefer to book it online? You can book an Osaka and Kyoto luggage delivery service, a partner link. Check the listing for pick-up points, sizes, prices and cut-off times.

Going on to Kyoto? Kyoto City runs Hands-Free Kyoto, which delivers luggage between stations and hotels in the city. See hands-free.kyoto.travel for the current drop-off points.

Sources: Kansai Airport: luggage and delivery counters and porter service, checked 18 November 2026. Hours, prices and counters change, and the airport's delivery page was last edited some time ago, so confirm at the counter.

Help for older travellers and anyone who needs assistance

You do not have to cope alone. Asking early is easier than struggling.

  • Tourist Information Center. Kansai Tourist Information Center is on the south side of Terminal 1, 1st floor, before security (9 am to 7 pm, phone +81 72-456-6160). Staff speak English, Chinese and Korean. They sell train tickets for foreign travellers and can book hotels.
  • Information counters. There is also an information counter on the 4th floor of Terminal 1. Staff can answer questions and solve problems.
  • Special assistance. The airport's service pages cover facilities for passengers who need assistance. To arrange help from the aircraft door to baggage claim or the station, contact your airline.
  • Porter service. A porter can carry your bags from the arrival gate (530 yen basic charge in Terminal 1, reserved by 11 am the day before).
  • Trains and buses. Tell station or bus staff if you need help.
  • Before you fly. Ask your airline to arrange assistance at the airport when you book.

Sources: Kansai Tourist Information Center and the airport's porter service page, checked 18 November 2026.

Leaving Kansai Airport

  • Know your terminal. Most airlines use Terminal 1. Peach, Jeju Air and Spring Airlines use Terminal 2. Check your ticket before you set off.
  • Taking the JR Kansai Airport Rapid to the airport? The train splits at Hineno on the way to the airport. JR West says to ride one of the first four cars.
  • Return rented gear first. If you rented a pocket Wi-Fi, check how to return it. Some companies take it back at the airport counter, others at a postbox.
  • Allow time. Check-in, security and passport control all take time, so arrive early. Your airline sets its own advice for international flights, so check your ticket.
  • Shopped tax-free? From 1 November 2026 you pay the tax at the shop and claim it back at the airport. Within 90 days of buying, scan your passport at a tax-free procedure terminal before you board, with your receipts and the items with you, because they may be inspected and cannot be checked in first. Ask at an information counter where the terminals are at Kansai Airport.
  • Cash left over? Currency exchange counters in Terminal 1 are on 1F, 3F (after immigration) and 4F.

Landing late or leaving very early?

Aeroplaza, the building joined to the station, has Hotel Nikko Kansai Airport and Cafe Lounge NODOKA, which is open 24 hours and has showers. Kansai Airport also keeps a page for late-night and early-morning passengers: see its information for late-night and early-morning flights. Check each hotel's own site for prices and check-in times.

🏨 Good for: families, groups and anyone on an early flight who wants an ordinary hotel room; the hotel says it is connected on foot to the terminal building. See rooms at Hotel Nikko Kansai Airport (partner link).

Frequently Asked Questions

Which airport do I use for Osaka and Kyoto?

Kansai International Airport (KIX) is the international airport for Osaka, Kyoto, Nara and Kobe. Kyoto has no airport of its own: from KIX the Haruka limited express takes about 75 minutes to Kyoto. Osaka International Airport (Itami, ITM) is for domestic flights only, and Kansai Airport's own site sends international travellers to KIX.

Which terminal will my flight use?

International flights arrive at Terminal 1, on the 1st floor. Peach, Jeju Air and Spring Airlines use Terminal 2. A free shuttle bus links the two terminals. Check your ticket or airline app, because airlines can change terminals.

How do I get from Kansai Airport to Osaka?

Kansai Airport lists about 65 minutes to Osaka Station by Kansai Airport Rapid (JR), about 50 minutes to Shin-Osaka by Haruka, and about 34 to 43 minutes to Namba by Nankai, depending on the train. The Airport Limousine Bus to Osaka Station (Umeda) costs 1,800 yen one way, according to the bus company, KATE. Fares and times change, so check the operator before you go.

How do I get from Kansai Airport to Kyoto?

The Haruka limited express runs from the airport to Kyoto Station in about 75 minutes. The Airport Limousine Bus to Kyoto costs 2,800 yen one way (KATE), and the bus takes about 1 hour 25 to 1 hour 30 minutes, depending on traffic.

Do I pay extra to ride the Haruka?

Yes. The Haruka needs a limited express ticket on top of the basic fare. JR West also sells a discounted one-way ticket called WEST QR HARUKA to visitors with Temporary Visitor status, through travel agencies. JR West's page listed 2,700 yen for Kyoto and 1,900 yen for Osaka or Shin-Osaka (adult) when we checked on 18 November 2026.

What is the Nankai Rapi:t, and do I need a special ticket?

Rapi:t is Nankai's reserved-seat express to Namba. It needs an extra limited express ticket (the upgrade is 520 yen on Nankai's page). The Nankai Airport Express needs only a normal ticket.

Is there free Wi-Fi at Kansai Airport?

Yes. Look for the network called AirportFreeWiFi-Kansai. The airport lists it for Terminal 1, the bus station, the railway station, Aeroplaza and Terminal 2, with a few gaps along the international arrival route. It is fine for quick messages, not for a full day of maps and apps.

Do I need Visit Japan Web?

It is not required, because you can still use paper forms. But registering on the free Visit Japan Web site before you fly lets you show QR codes for immigration and customs, which can speed up your arrival.

Where can I buy an ICOCA card at Kansai Airport?

The airport says ICOCA is sold at the JR or Nankai Kansai Airport Station, outside Terminal 1 on the 2nd floor. JR West ticket machines sell ICOCA too. We did not find an official page saying Welcome Suica is sold at Kansai Airport.

Is there a prayer room at Kansai Airport?

Yes. The airport lists prayer rooms in Terminal 1, on the 3rd floor and in the international gate area, with separate ablution spaces for men and women. Anyone can use them.

Can I take a shower at Kansai Airport?

Yes. After security, coin-operated showers are in the middle of the international gate area on 2F of Terminal 1 (800 yen for 15 minutes, 7 am to 11 pm). Before security, Cafe Lounge NODOKA on Aeroplaza 2F has showers open 24 hours (1,400 yen for 40 minutes, amenity set included). Hours and prices can change.

Can I send my suitcase to my hotel?

Yes. Yamato and JAL ABC run baggage counters in Terminal 1, and the airport lists a Kansai Airport Baggage Service counter on 1F as well. There is no counter in Terminal 2. Ask the counter for the price and delivery day before you hand your bag over.
