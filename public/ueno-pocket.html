Which level are you on?

Ueno Park: 3F (Park Gate, Panda Bridge). Taxi and ticket office: 1F (Central Gate).

3F

Park level

Park Gate, Panda Bridge Exit, Iriya Gate. ecute Ueno shops inside the gates. Statue of Panda

🌸 Ueno Park: Park Gate or Panda Bridge

🛗Lifts A to F: 3F to platforms 1 to 12
2F

JR tracks 1 to 12

Yamanote Line 2 and 3, Keihin-Tohoku 1 and 4, Ueno-Tokyo Line 7 and 8. Lost & Found by the Panda Bridge Exit stairs

⚠️ Narita Line 10 to 12: Narita city, not the airport

1F

Ground level

Central Gate, Main Exit, taxi stop, ticket office, Travel Service Center. Asakusa, Yamashita and Iriya exits. JR tracks 13 to 17. M2 floor: Shinobazu Gate

🚇 Tokyo Metro: “for Tokyo Metro” signs by the Central Gate

🚅Transfer to Shinkansen gate (1F), lifts G and H
B

Underground, B1 to B4

Shinkansen tracks 19 to 22. 19 and 20: Tohoku, Yamagata, Akita, Hokkaido, Joetsu, Hokuriku. 21 and 22: for Tokyo. Waiting room
