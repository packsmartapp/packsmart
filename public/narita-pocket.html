Need help?

Every terminal has information counters. Look for the "?" sign. Staff will help, so just ask.

After landing

  1. Immigration. Have your passport ready. Follow the signs and staff.
  2. Baggage claim. Find your flight on the screens.
  3. Customs. Show your Visit Japan Web QR code, or a paper form if your flight gave you one.
  4. Arrivals hall. SIM and Wi-Fi counters, cash machines, convenience stores and train tickets.

Free Wi-Fi for quick messages: connect to FreeWiFi-NARITA. Visit Japan Web needs internet, so fill it in before you fly and take a screenshot of the QR codes.

Keep this on your phone

Add it to your home screen once, while you have signal. It then opens like an app, even with no internet.

  • iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
  • Android: in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".

Floors, opening hours and counters can change. If you cannot find something, ask at an information counter. Found something out of date? Tell us (needs internet). The full guide, with trains, SIM and luggage, is at packsmartapp.com (needs internet).
