Which terminal are you in?

Tap what you need. Not sure of your terminal? Choose "Not sure" to see all three.

Check your ticket or airline app: airlines can change terminals.

  1. ImmigrationHave your passport ready. Follow the signs and staff.
  2. Baggage claimFind your flight on the screens.
  3. CustomsShow your Visit Japan Web QR code, or the paper form if your flight gave you one.
  4. Arrivals hallSIM and Wi-Fi counters, cash machines, convenience stores and train tickets.

Free Wi-Fi for quick messages: connect to FreeWiFi-NARITA. Visit Japan Web needs internet, so fill it in before you fly and take a screenshot of the QR codes.

Keep this on your phone

Add it to your home screen once, while you have signal. It then opens like an app, even with no internet.

iPhone: in Safari, tap Share, then "Add to Home Screen".
Android: in Chrome, tap the menu (three dots), then "Add to Home screen" or "Install app".
